**IPC-001 release.** Independent Release approved local synchronous call, receive and reply on single-core AArch64 QEMU virt FIFO and RR with two or three configured processes, directed call rights and an eight-byte request/reply. The twelve original boots and one additional round-robin boot cover both arrival orders, donation and restoration, and cleanup. Exact-time caller death and reply order were checked on the host. **IPC-002 release · IMP-303 · EVD-303.** Independent Release approved the two-app native mailbox on single-core AArch64 QEMU virt FIFO and RR, bringing #24 to 2/10. Twelve guest boots have retained raw evidence. Two additional release boots, one per policy, covered finite-deadline sender promotion and empty receive. The first strict fixture used unsupported relative sleep and exited 84/83. The corrected absolute-sleep fixture exited 71/72. Two transient captures lack retained raw data. Exact deadline equality, two waiting senders and forced exit of a blocked sender were host cases. Physical and functional safety qualification remain open. **IPC-003 release · IMP-304 · EVD-304.** Independent Release approved notifications between two ordinary native apps on single-core AArch64 QEMU virt FIFO and RR, bringing #24 to 3/10. Nine of ten original boots matched expectations. One fixture omitted the actual EACCES result from its expectations, and a separate eleventh boot used the corrected expectation. An additional RR release boot reversed the grants and exited 75/76. Duplicate pulses delivered two records, while repeated sets of one event bit produced one pending bit. Exact deadline equality, forced exit and all-bit coverage were host cases. **IPC-004 release · IMP-305 · EVD-305.** Independent Release approved native FIFO/RR waitsets on single-core AArch64 QEMU virt. This was the fourth family accepted under #24 (4/10). Sixteen first cold boots were followed by two independently rebuilt release boots, one per policy. Both returned app exits 71/72 with zero dropped scheduler rows. Cross-checking 33 scheduler rows against private RAM supported nonconsuming readiness, wake on an actual send, stale-handle rejection after slot reuse, and timeout status −110. Exact deadline equality, forced death and the generation cap were host checks. Physical and general certification remain outside this approval. **IPC-005 release · IMP-306 · EVD-306.** Independent Release approved CPU-only shared-buffer loans on single-core Cortex-A72 QEMU virt under FIFO and RR. This is the fifth scoped family approval under #24 (5/10). The 11 boots included one disabled control. Across 21 RAM captures and the ten enabled boots, the dedicated loan journal retained all 148 ownership transitions. In the two polling boots the generic scheduler journal dropped 2,828 FIFO events and 2,611 RR events. Each dedicated journal retained all 34 transitions. Guest cleanup exercised ordinary process exit. Guest fault or forced-kill recovery, physical cross-core visibility, DMA, WCET and ASIL qualification remain outside this approval. ## Current release scope

**Six of seven retained scheduling families have scoped approvals (6/7).** POL-002 fixed priority remains open for both FIFO and round robin (RR). Existing approvals cover POL-003 batch (named native and linked profiles), POL-005 deadlines (finite M1/M3 EDF and sampled LLF), POL-009 multilevel queues (telemetry deployment), POL-010 proportional share (six native configurations), POL-011 weighted scheduling (named configurations), and POL-012 EEVDF (linked EL1 telemetry).

REQ-023 retains all eighteen families and their historical evidence. POL-001, POL-004, POL-006 through POL-008 and POL-013 through POL-018 are catalog-only for this release. Physical Pi 4 verification under [#34](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/34), customer workload and WCET qualification, and ASIL qualification remain open. Under CHG-302, the verified #363 virtual FIFO/RR playback authorizes IPC implementation under #24. At opening, 0/10 IPC families had been accepted. ## Profile and verification record

Freeze enabled families, directions, rights, deadlines, ordering, overflow behavior and QoS per endpoint. Record maximum message/record size, pool counts and byte totals, per-core state, copy count, wakeup cost and maximum fan-out. Charge queues and loans to an owner. Specify admission failure before allocating, and bound teardown and deferred reclamation. Define a commit point for each blocking operation. Race timeout, cancellation, close and peer death on both sides of that point. Count a committed operation once and return unused capacity. Publish kernel RAM and image deltas against REQ-009, including reserve pools.

For VER-024 a fresh verifier pins revision, configuration, topology, capacities and expected outcomes before execution, checks the frozen image digest, then replays each selected case with concurrent endpoints. Record actual outcomes, timing, peak occupancy and post-teardown counts with raw traces and artifact hashes. Mark omitted families and their profiles explicitly. Time bounds, cross-core interference and DMA claims require applicable board measurements. Linux-domain network profiles identify the unmodified driver and its domain; a Linux binary socket interface requires its own declared ABI coverage.
