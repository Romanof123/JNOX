# IPC mechanisms and profile acceptance

[한국어](ipc-mechanisms.md) · English

**IPC-001 release.** Independent Release approved local synchronous call, receive and reply on single-core AArch64 QEMU virt FIFO and RR with two or three configured processes, directed call rights and an eight-byte request/reply. The twelve original boots and one additional round-robin boot cover both arrival orders, donation and restoration, and cleanup. Exact-time caller death and reply order were checked on the host. [Release decision](evidence/native-sync-ipc-release.en-ko.md) · [Supplemental raw data](evidence/native-sync-ipc-release-supplement.tar.gz) · [Live family decisions](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/24).

## Current release scope

**Six of seven retained scheduling families have scoped approvals (6/7).** POL-002 fixed priority remains open for both FIFO and round robin (RR). Existing approvals cover POL-003 batch (named native and linked profiles), POL-005 deadlines (finite M1/M3 EDF and sampled LLF), POL-009 multilevel queues (telemetry deployment), POL-010 proportional share (six native configurations), POL-011 weighted scheduling (named configurations), and POL-012 EEVDF (linked EL1 telemetry).

REQ-023 retains all eighteen families and their historical evidence. POL-001, POL-004, POL-006 through POL-008 and POL-013 through POL-018 are catalog-only for this release. Physical Pi 4 verification under [#34](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/34), customer workload and WCET qualification, and ASIL qualification remain open. Under CHG-302, the verified #363 virtual FIFO/RR playback authorizes IPC implementation under #24. At opening, 0/10 IPC families had been accepted. **CHG-007 → REQ-024 → DES-013 → VER-024.** The ten IPC families are planned under the following release sequence.

| ID | Scope, ownership and service contract | Bound and discriminating acceptance case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IPC-001 | Local synchronous send/receive/reply. A caller blocks until a server consumes its single-use reply token and replies, or cancellation wins. Donate effective priority along authorized fixed-priority call chains, reject cycles and unwind on every terminal path. | Cap waiters, message bytes and chain depth. Place a medium-priority runner between a high caller and low server. Race reply against timeout and caller death. Require one terminal outcome, restored priorities and rejection of a late token. |
| IPC-002 | Local asynchronous mailbox. A successful send copies one whole message into endpoint-owned FIFO storage. At capacity, return would-block or wait to a deadline. | Cap slots, payload and blocked senders. Fill N slots, time out sender N+1, drain in order and verify that its message never appears. |
| IPC-003 | Local pulse/event. A pulse enqueues a bounded record and reports full. A bit event latches pending bits and coalesces repeated sets until consumed. The endpoint owns pending state. | Cap pulse slots and event bits. Signal twice before receipt. Expect two pulses given two free slots and one pending bit for repeated sets. |
| IPC-004 | Local waitset. Authorized registrations observe level readiness of endpoints without consuming data. Wakeups require a readiness recheck. Detach invalidates the registration generation. | Cap registrations, scan and ready batch. Race readiness with enrollment and detach/reuse. Check no missed persistent readiness or stale delivery to the replacement. |
| IPC-005 | Local shared memory and fixed-buffer loans. Restrict mappings and give one writer a loan until publish transfers ownership. A single-producer/single-consumer ring uses release/acquire publication, bounded occupancy and would-block on full. Reclaim only after outstanding access ends. | Cap pages, loans and ring slots. Wrap indices, kill a holder and reject stale return after reuse. For cross-core use check visibility on the board. For DMA mappings check device completion and platform cache clean/invalidate and barrier rules before recycling. |
| IPC-006 | Socket endpoints. Local stream preserves byte order, datagram preserves message boundaries, and connected seqpacket preserves record boundaries and order. Network TCP stream and UDP datagram use a separate network transport. Bound buffers, backlog and payload. Stream writes may be partial, including at backpressure. Full local datagram/record queues refuse or wait for a whole record. | Send two records, split stream reads, and verify record boundaries in record modes. Close a stream writer and drain to EOF. Exercise TCP peer reset and UDP loss/reordering without promising delivery. Test only the declared address/transport profile. |
| IPC-007 | Local anonymous pipe or named FIFO. Open handles own each end of a bounded byte stream. An empty live pipe blocks or returns would-block. The last writer closing yields EOF after drain. A write with no reader fails. | Fix capacity and atomic-write limit. Compete with two writers within the limit, fill the pipe, close duplicated ends and check atomicity and exact EOF. Include the native C pipeline where enabled. |
| IPC-008 | Publish/subscribe service. An authorized publisher fans out to bounded per-subscriber queues. A full subscriber queue drops that subscriber’s newest sample and increments its loss count. Unsubscribe retires its generation. Local service by default. Remote bridging requires a declared transport profile. | Cap topics, subscribers, sample bytes and delivery work per activation. Stall one subscriber and verify its loss count, ordered delivery to a draining subscriber and no stale delivery after re-subscribe. |
| IPC-009 | Local capability transfer and lifetime. Specify copy or move and attenuate rights. Commit receiver installation and move-source invalidation atomically. A failed transfer retains source ownership. Close/death releases references. Generation checks reject stale handles. | Cap handles, rights descriptors and transfers per message. Fill the receiver table and kill a peer during move. Check rollback or a single committed owner, rights attenuation and stale rejection after slot reuse. |
| IPC-010 | Safety end-to-end channel, local or through an explicitly authorized relay. The receiver owns validation and freshness, and the relay forwards protected bytes unchanged. Bind endpoint, data ID and generation. Check integrity, sequence, issue time and validity before control use. CRC detects accidental corruption. Authenticity against a malicious peer requires a separate authenticated mechanism and key lifecycle. | Cap queue depth and receive work per activation. Saturate with invalid frames and deliver a valid frame exactly at expiry. Check expiry precedence, unchanged accepted state on rejection, inhibition and new-generation rearm against [ADR-001](safety-parameters.en.md) and [REQ-019](requirements.en.md). |

**EVD-302 · IMP-302 (#365).** Twelve first cold boots on single-core QEMU virt met their expected outcomes on `6f51e0b1`. In the four caller-first and receiver-first FIFO/RR boots, private C/Rust RAM showed reply `0x000404010101082e` and unknown, valid and duplicate token statuses `−116 / 0 / −116`. Separate guest cases observed donation ordering, recovery after timeout, server exit and resource reclamation. Exact-time races and caller death with slot reuse were host observations. Family release review is pending. ## Profile and verification record

Freeze enabled families, directions, rights, deadlines, ordering, overflow behavior and QoS per endpoint. Record maximum message/record size, pool counts and byte totals, per-core state, copy count, wakeup cost and maximum fan-out. Charge queues and loans to an owner. Specify admission failure before allocating, and bound teardown and deferred reclamation. Define a commit point for each blocking operation. Race timeout, cancellation, close and peer death on both sides of that point. Count a committed operation once and return unused capacity. Publish kernel RAM and image deltas against REQ-009, including reserve pools.

For VER-024 a fresh verifier pins revision, configuration, topology, capacities and expected outcomes before execution, checks the frozen image digest, then replays each selected case with concurrent endpoints. Record actual outcomes, timing, peak occupancy and post-teardown counts with raw traces and artifact hashes. Mark omitted families and their profiles explicitly. Time bounds, cross-core interference and DMA claims require applicable board measurements. Linux-domain network profiles identify the unmodified driver and its domain; a Linux binary socket interface requires its own declared ABI coverage.
