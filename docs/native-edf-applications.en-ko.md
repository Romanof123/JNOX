# EDF for independently packaged native jobs / 독립 네이티브 작업의 EDF 스케줄링

CHG-117 / IMP-117 / EVD-117 reserved. Prospective design for #21 POL-005 on accepted main `3195c27b4984af3bca64f948fac49b8ec773e9a1`. An operator supplies separately linked C or Rust programs, when each may start, its due time and its estimated CPU demand. A released job with an earlier deadline can interrupt a computing peer on CPU0. The interrupted process later continues in its own address space. The linked EDF/LLF tasks remain the comparison profiles. [POL-005](scheduling-release-cases.en.md) · [Existing finite reference](ordinary-deadline-plan.md)

운영자는 독립 ELF마다 시작 가능 시각·절대 마감·예상 CPU 수요를 지정한다. CPU0에서 계산 중인 작업보다 마감이 이른 작업이 도착하면 실제 타이머를 통해 선점하고, 먼저 실행하던 앱은 자기 주소 공간과 스택에서 재개한다. 기존 연결형 EDF·LLF와 새 네이티브 작업은 각각의 기준으로 판정한다.

## Package and admission / 패키지와 수용 판정

Proposed Linux/WSL command, from the repository root with the three SDK-built inputs present:

~~~sh
python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out edf-three --policy edf \
    --elf batch.elf --job 0,8,2 \
    --peer-elf histogram.elf --peer-job 0,12,8 \
    --peer-elf urgent.elf --peer-job 1,4,1
~~~

Each tuple is `release,deadline,cost`, three unsigned decimal integers in grid units. Deadlines are absolute offsets from the common boot epoch S. The finite reference profile has 1–3 slots, Q = CNTFRQ/10 exactly (100 ms), 0 ≤ release < deadline ≤ 16, cost > 0, unrestricted supply. One primary tuple and one peer tuple per independently verified ELF, in peer-argument order. Existing primary `--source` remains available. Enforce selector exclusivity and exact tuple counts at CLI and raw Cargo configuration, rejecting incompatible priorities, weights and quantum options. Record the profile identity, units, tuples and each ELF digest in slot order. Preserve startup, cooperative, FIFO/RR and other existing selectors. The 16-grid limit belongs to this explicitly selected admission profile; it sets no general native process lifetime or system scheduling limit.

Separate the numeric job descriptor and finite demand/simulation admission from the linked entry-point and milliseconds checks in `deadline_jobs.rs`. Keep the original wrapper and masked-supply callers, including their existing errors. For native admission validate the complete set of ELF load plans, stacks and mappings before any user entry. Apply the same endpoint-demand sums and sampled EDF simulation with zero withheld supply; model completion exactly at deadline passes. Check frequency conversion, horizon and S+kQ with checked arithmetic. Perform substantial loading and preparation before selecting a future common S. Report any rejected table, arithmetic failure or epoch already missed at arm; never start a subset. Actual deadline achievement is measured from the sampled counter. The simulation assumes nonsuspending independent jobs and their declared costs. [Admission and model](../kernel/src/deadline_jobs.rs) · [Ordinary package](../tools/build_native_el0.py)

튜플은 공통 시점 기준의 공개 시각·절대 마감·예상 비용이다. 이 유한 수용 프로필은 100 ms 단위 16칸 안에서 최대 세 작업을 다룬다. 주 앱과 동료별 튜플을 정확히 대응시키고 ELF 해시와 함께 기록한다. 기존 수용 모델의 구간별 수요와 모의 EDF 완료 검사를 공유하고 기존 연결형 작업의 함수·실행 길이 검사는 보존한다. 모델은 스스로 차단하지 않는 독립 작업에 대한 조건부 결과다. 실측 판정은 실제 카운터로 한다.

## CPU0 dispatch / 실행과 복귀

Use the normal EL0 loader, per-process address space, saved frame, SDK exit, absolute sleep and yield. Use `DeadlineScheduler<N>` as sole EDF selector. It already provides stable ready order, running job, release, completion, sampled selection and signed LLF laxity. Factor the ordinary incumbent-preserving selection into the shared path for these native jobs too. Convert each observed physical counter to k=floor((counter−S)/Q) after epoch entry; the existing policy takes logical indices with quantum 1. Arm physical PPI30 to the next relevant release/grid or absolute sleeper deadline, whichever comes first. Delay all first entries until release; idle when nothing is ready. Record nominal and observed times separately. At a late IRQ charge the actual incumbent over the observed interval once and admit every due release in nominal-time then slot order. Make one decision at the observed grid index. Never replay imaginary owner switches at skipped boundaries. A skipped required sample disqualifies its scored run. [Shared selector](../kernel/src/deadline_policy.rs) · [Linked dispatcher](../kernel/src/ordinary_deadline.rs) · [Native dispatcher](../kernel/src/native_el0.rs)

At a scheduling boundary account the outgoing interval, apply the caller's state transition, admit due releases/wakes, then select. Expose explicit shared-policy suspend, resume and yield operations, preserving each job's original absolute deadline and remaining estimate. A release enters once; resume enqueues an existing job. For same nominal time, initial releases precede resumes, with slot order within each group. Preserve the incumbent on equal deadlines during ordinary sampled arbitration. Yield drops the incumbent to the back of its equal-deadline group, and immediately dispatches the earliest ready deadline; a sole or earlier-deadline yielding job can win again. A future absolute sleep blocks only its caller. An already-due sleep returns immediately. A mid-grid wake makes the job ready; idle CPUs select immediately, while an active incumbent is displaced involuntarily only at the next sampled arbitration. Service an exact-boundary wake before that boundary's decision. Preserve at most one grid-driven involuntary change per observed index; explicit exit/block/yield are separate. Complete only on actual SDK exit. A live job may keep running after cost exhaustion or after a miss; record both and its eventual result. A blocked overdue job retains its deadline when it wakes. Keep original EDF/LLF tie rules, cost floor and admission controls for the old callers.

Record raw service intervals, excluding idle and scheduler gaps, independently of logical model cost (which clamps at one while live). Gate all private pointer access by current slot, restore SP/registers/PC and activate the selected mappings on every switch. Keep events bounded for scored captures with explicit truncation; completing ordinary programs must not depend on trace capacity.

일반 EL0 로더·프로세스별 프레임과 기존 DeadlineScheduler를 사용한다. 물리 카운터를 실제 관측한 격자 번호로 변환한다. 늦은 인터럽트에서는 실제 실행자에게 한 번만 정산하고 도착한 작업을 순서대로 넣는다. 같은 마감에서는 기존 실행자를 유지한다. 자발적 양보는 동률 그룹 뒤로 보내고, 절대 대기·복귀는 원래 마감과 남은 비용을 보존한다. 격자 사이에 깨어나면 유휴 CPU에서는 즉시 선택하고 실행자가 있으면 다음 격자의 선점 판정에 참여한다. 모델 비용 소진·실제 마감 지연·자발적 중단과 실제 종료를 구별한다.

## Workload and first observations / 작업과 최초 관측

Use single Cortex-A72 QEMU virt/GICv2, 128 MiB, stock clock and ordinary SDK. Freeze package/source hashes, compiler, app algorithms and inputs, epoch rule, first plain and external exception-traced positions and replay before scored runs. Obtain private symbols and instruction ranges from each linked ELF. Three finite sensor reductions supply independently recomputable results:

| Slot | Job, (release, due, cost) | Application result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | C batch checksum (0,8,2) | Repeatedly process blocks of 4,096 byte samples x(i)=(17i+3) mod 256. Accumulate a private u64 sum and a block count. Finish after a complete block once at least 160 ms elapsed since A began; cap at 65,536 blocks with explicit failure if exhausted early. Each block sums to 522,240. |
| B | Rust histogram (0,12,8) | Count the high nibble of the same byte sequence for 128 blocks. Each of 16 private bins must equal 32,768; return success after verifying the bins. |
| C | C urgent alarm reduction (1,4,1) | Count x(i)≥240 in eight blocks. Require 2,048 exceedances and publish that count before exit. |

For A, use ordinary monotonic reads between complete blocks and volatile input/progress so the compiler retains useful work; qualify interruption inside the user reduction from exception PC and linked disassembly. Publish products and count in private writable storage for a post-run capture. Use distinct successful exit statuses (17, 29, 43 respectively) and separate error codes. A's 160 ms wall-time minimum is a workload stimulus, independent of its modeled 200 ms cost. Avoid workload calibration against the scored first result. If a frozen workload ends early or an IRQ lands in a clock call, retain that first result and its qualification limits.

1. **Released urgent job.** For the table above, independently reproduce modeled C/A/B completion at grids 2/3/11. At real S dispatch A before B. At S+Q the release IRQ must find A live in its own reduction; observe A→C, C exit, restored A frame and continued private progress, A exit, then B. Compare all three actual exit stamps with S+8Q, S+12Q and S+4Q respectively. A successful estimate model alone gives no measured pass. Record each timer sample's lateness and compare, grid or skipped indices, all actual switches and adjacent physical dwell. Apply EVD-048's per-index switch accounting; product interference bounds remain open.
2. **Independent scheduling controls.** Freeze equal-deadline jobs to demonstrate incumbent retention and package order on equal releases; use a first release at grid 1 to observe idle→job. Cost 2 with release 0/deadline 1 must be refused before entry. Model equality (0,1,1) is admissible. Exercise a live single A with cost 1, deadline 8: real raw service greater than Q, positive later progress and exactly one exit. Separately force a measured deadline miss (0,1,1) with A's minimum 160 ms and record its eventual valid product and exit. Test one-count-late/equality deadline classification, conversion overflow, duplicates and malformed tuples independently.
3. **Suspension and yield.** Use two equal-deadline jobs released at 0 and due at 10. One yields into its peer, then exercise both asleep with distinct future SDK absolute deadlines; freeze their requests so the second remains blocked at the first wake. Check zero service while blocked, idle→first wake, unchanged job deadline/cost at resume, preserved private data and final exit. Add a sole-survivor yield, elapsed sleep and mid-grid earlier-deadline wake with a running later-deadline peer to exercise deferred sampled preemption. Score syscall results, timing and ownership as functional behavior; this self-suspending run is outside the nonsuspending admission timing claim.

Retain ELF identities and mappings; event counter/compare, logical release/deadline, ready/blocked IDs, selected owners, logical remaining cost, uncapped actual service, exit stamps and private products; saved/restored frames/SP/ELR and external exception order. Preserve prior linked EDF and sampled LLF comparisons under their original recipes, including LLF crossover, ties and negative slack. #178's historical weighted performance, #21's integrated interference and physical-board decision keep their existing dispositions. 최초 일반·외부 추적 부팅 전에 바이너리·명령·입력과 판정식을 동결한다. A는 4,096바이트마다 522,240을 더하고 B는 16개 구간별 32,768개, 긴급 C는 임계치 초과 2,048개를 자체 계산한다. 실제 격자 1에서 계산 중인 A를 C가 선점하고 C 종료 뒤 A의 PC·스택·계산이 이어져야 한다. 각 작업의 실제 종료 시각을 각 절대 마감과 대조한다. 별도 사례에서 동률·유휴·수용 거부·비용 초과·실측 마감 실패를 구분한다. 양보·절대 대기·전체 유휴·복귀는 기능으로 판정하며 비차단 작업의 수용 보장과 섞지 않는다. 기존 LLF의 음의 여유·교차·동률 검사 및 #21의 실물·통합 부하 판정은 그대로 유지한다.
