## Interface / 운영자 인터페이스

Use `python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out out/amc --policy amc --elf low.elf --peer-elf monitor.elf --peer-elf low2.elf --peer-elf survivor.elf --amc-manifest deployment.json`. Support 1–4 slots; only AMC gains a fourth private image, saved frame, MMU root and startup stack. Each ordered descriptor declares LO/HI tier, fixed priority 1–255 (higher wins, low ID breaks ties), LO/HI allowances, period T, relative deadline D, first offset, generation count 1–16 and per-generation normal argv. All durations/offsets are integer cells; 0<C_LO≤C_HI≤D≤T, LO allowances equal. A finite restart list has at most 16 strictly ordered cell indices with explicit operator approval flags; default denies. No requested execution demand. Reject unknown fields, zero/invalid budgets, mismatched slots, conflicting policies, duplicate resets, out-of-range inputs and grid arithmetic overflow before any EL0 return. CLI and raw build paths enforce identical constraints. Bind input and ordered ELF hashes, argv, decoded parameters and roots to each package.

슬롯별 등급·우선순위·두 예산·주기·상대 마감·최초 릴리스·세대 수와 각 argv를 선언한다. 0<C_LO≤C_HI≤D≤T, LO 양 예산 동일, 동순위 낮은 ID 우선이다. 운영자의 재시작 허가 목록은 별도이며 기본 거부다. 이미지·범위·산술 및 설정 오류를 패키저와 직접 빌드에서 모두 거부한다.

## Enforcement / 예산 집행

One CPU0 virt A72/GICv2, default TCG, 128 MiB, F=CNTFRQ, Q=floor(F/20)>0, one future epoch E. Release n at E+(offset+nT)Q, absolute deadline release+DQ. Use real PPI30, full frame and TTBR restoration. Charge disjoint physical return-to-next-exception intervals in ticks; report pre-ERET/entry uncertainty, raw charge, counter cutoff and late spill separately. Exclude idle, sleeping, preemption and kernel work. Preserve residuals across ordinary short SVCs. Arm the earliest budget, deadline, release, wake or reset event. Never rewrite scheduled times after late entry. Use the shared domain's discard, positive-grant and quiescent-reset semantics with a native adapter; synthetic remaining demand cannot choose completion or trigger transition.

At each decision settle outgoing charge, honor an actual completion inside entitlement, apply budget/deadline decisions, then reset attempt, due releases and dispatch. Complete at or before the LO allowance without mode change. A HI job actually reaching its LO allowance with unfinished work enters HI on its first positive continued grant. On exhaustion retire/fault the generation and deny further service; at C_HI allow no fourth grant in the original 1/3 case. Report every measured overrun without silently capping occupancy. Before return recheck applicable eligibility and cutoff. Overlapping releases (even for blocked jobs), expired generations and LO admission in HI must never clobber a live frame. A deadline miss latches a fault without silently completing the generation. An overdue job remains accountable until completion, exhaustion or criticality discard. Missed historical releases are reported with their original deadlines; no retroactive service.

At LO→HI freeze every LO generation's charged/remaining allowance, last private product, sleep and saved frame; cancel further LO grants, wake and return before HI dispatch. Preserve every surviving HI frame, stack, data, absolute deadline and residual allowance. Capture terminal/discard products before rebuilding the slot from the pristine ELF, stack/TLS and new argv. Tag every admitted generation and refuse stale operations. An approved reset in HI requires the entire domain quiescent, including sleepers; reject reset with pending work, in LO or without authority. Idle alone never changes mode. Record all reset/refusal events and preserve failures across a successful restart.

실제 복귀→예외 구간을 중복 없이 차감하고 선점·수면·커널 시간을 제외한다. 예산·마감·릴리스·깨움·재시작 중 가장 이른 시각에 비교기를 설정한다. 실제 연속 HI 승인이 LO 예산을 넘을 때 전환하며, 폐기한 LO의 프레임에 다시 복귀하지 않는다. 일부 실행된 LO의 마지막 결과와 이월량을 보존하고 생존 HI의 세대·잔여 예산·절대 마감·데이터를 복원한다. 예산 초과와 물리 IRQ 지연을 구별해 기록한다. 새 세대는 초기 ELF 데이터로 시작한다. 잠든 HI를 포함해 작업이 남았거나 승인되지 않은 재시작은 거부한다.

## Finite fixtures / 유한 작업

Reuse the linked telemetry ring [2,5,1,6,3,7,4,8] in ordinary native SDK worker ELFs. Independent oracle for ID i, accepted generation g and n samples: start at (i+g) mod 8, sum full rings as 36 each plus the prefix, modulo 2^64. Stream jobs actually accumulate readings in 256-sample batches until a requested observation duration in CPU milliseconds; publish coherent counts, sums and task stamps. Provide normal CLOCK_PROCESS_CPUTIME_ID through clock_gettime using the same documented generation clock, including the current partial interval. No predeclared demand, busy delay or work synthesized by the scheduler. The finite reducer stores exactly eight or sixteen readings and sums; a long survivor additionally publishes its eight-reading checkpoint before streaming and final sixteen-reading result afterwards. Success exits with slot ID. Freeze all ELF hashes and options before first execution.

Use Q=3,125,000 ticks at F=62.5 MHz. Priorities for IDs 0..3 are 1,4,2,3. Single-generation T=16 unless stated. Numeric durations are app inputs. Owners may change immediately upon native completion. Keep the original linked exact-LO/exact-HI/over-HI and staggered schedules and timing gates under their original decoders.

The staggered native initial and carry-in LO jobs occupy separate slots so each keeps its declared deadline/period; the original linked test keeps both LO0 generations. The named carry-in is one partially serviced LO plus a ready LO in the four-live pair, and one partially serviced LO plus a partially serviced HI survivor in staggered. Enumerate each generation's actual remaining ticks at crossing. This envelope does not bound unrelated product arrivals.

Refusal control: HI1 at b0 (1/3,D4,T6; 110 ms), LO0 at b0,b1,b2,b3 (1/1,D1,T1; finite 8). At b2 reject approved reset while HI pending and refuse the new LO. At b3 refuse unapproved reset; at b4 accept approved reset if quiescent. Separate overlap control in LO: high-priority LO1 at b0 (2/2,D3,T3; 75 ms) and lower-priority LO0 (1/1,D1,T1; finite 8 at b0,b1). At b1 latch the pending LO0 deadline miss and reject its new generation. Freeze malformed-budget and fifth-image prelaunch refusals as well.

For every workload preserve first plain and separate first externally traced cold boots. Record raw PPI/IAR and actual CVAL, epoch, task-owned timed observations and SVC after publication, rows/drops, saved/returned PC/SP/root/generation, private RAM checks and signed exits or fault. For b=E+2Q in the four-live pair and E+5Q in staggered require b≤actual crossing IRQ i≤mode commit c≤first HI task observation x<b+Q. Report x−b and release→completion ticks/ns, actual compare relative to b and conservative timestamp uncertainty. Service suspension excludes kernel time so the compare can differ from the grid b. Check each unchanged deadline against actual publication and completion. Any missed first or insufficient observation remains visible. Do not force a late IRQ or substitute a retry.

입력은 위 표의 슬롯별 시각·예산·마감·CPU 밀리초로 고정한다. 실제 링 값을 256개씩 누적하고 세대별 시작점과 독립 계산식으로 진척을 확인한다. 전환 전후 실제 네 슬롯 점유, LO 폐기 마스크와 부분 결과, HI의 저장 프레임 복원, 8/36·16/72 및 마감 이전 완료 SVC를 검증한다. b2 또는 b5에서 다음 경계 전까지 교차 IRQ→커밋→앱 자체 HI 표본 순서를 요구한다. 비승인·활성 상태 재시작, HI 중 LO 릴리스와 LO 중 중복 세대 거부도 일반 입력으로 검증한다. 최초 일반·추적 자료, 누락과 실패를 그대로 남긴다.
