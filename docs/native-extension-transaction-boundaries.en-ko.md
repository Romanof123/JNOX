Prospective supplement to [#345](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/345) and [POL-018](scheduling-policies.en.md), based on 19adfb48b7daf12beb4be65d366e91537a2d2085. Preserve the twelve original native cold boots: 102 cells, all 74 delegated replies timely, zero cancellations, skips or dropped rows. Reserved, fallback and idle work and the private outputs have their original verdicts. The linked stopped-evaluator diagnostic exercised its own stopped progress, fallback and idle. 기존 네이티브 최초 부팅 12건의 측정은 그대로 둔다. 102셀 중 위임 응답 74건은 모두 기한 전에 게시됐고 취소·건너뜀·행 누락은 각각 0건이었다. 연료를 소진한 정상 응답에도 평가기 하트비트가 진전한다. 종료 시 비교기 해제, 연결형 정지 시험, 네이티브 미게시 거래 취소는 각각 다른 사건이다.

## Frozen deterministic controls / 고정된 결정론적 대조

Use the same allocation-free transitions called by the production EL1 evaluator and PPI30 consumer for staging, validating, taking, abandoning and committing replies. If extraction is needed, keep the physical counter sample after staging while IRQs are masked, the single-consumer ownership and the existing interrupt/return path. Keep the ordinary interpreter, group gate and Extension::decide in the host cases. Drive the shared transitions with explicit cell identities, counters, real proposal bytecode and declared eligible masks. A second implementation of the predicates cannot qualify the production transitions. [Native producer and consumer](../kernel/src/native_extension.rs) · [Watchdog and reservation precedence](../kernel/src/extension_policy.rs)

| Case | Expected production state |
|---|---|
| Matching active delegated cell k; publication at cutoff−1 | Stage a complete decision on the copy. Trusted state remains unchanged until the due PPI30 consumer takes it. Commit the copied policy and the single next heartbeat once. A repeated consume cannot grant again. |
| Matching k at cutoff and cutoff+1; absent reply | Reject publication at both times. At consumption mark cancellation and arbitrate the original opening snapshot with the unchanged trusted heartbeat. Assert the old nomination and copied state cannot escape; check lowest eligible delegate and no-eligible real-idle selection result separately. |
| Obsolete k after an opening change, inactive evaluation, and duplicate old consume | Refuse the old/inactive reply. Check trusted state, current-cell identity and heartbeat before and after. A stale producer must not erase a valid reply already staged for the newer transaction. Exercise both an empty newer transaction and a newer transaction with a valid reply. Terminal cleanup has no subsequent grant. |
| Heartbeat and independent service | Start with a confirmed heartbeat at delegated cell 0. Leave it unchanged at cell 1 and observe fallback without an elapsed two-cell watchdog. Reserved cell 2 bypasses evaluation and heartbeat publication and serves its eligible reservation, or idles if unavailable. At delegated cell 3 unchanged progress yields Stalled. Separately use a confirmed heartbeat at cell 1, reserved bypass at 2 and delegated expiry exactly at 3. Check an increased heartbeat resets progress age, and the existing regression rejection preserves the trusted counter. |

At Q=6,250,000 on the adopted virtual machine, publication cutoff is S+1,562,500 ticks. Retain fuel five, one-cell maximum grant and two-cell inclusive watchdog. Distinguish a producer arriving at cutoff from a consumer taking an earlier valid publication at cutoff. The host result records each input and actual reason, grant, stored reply identity and trusted heartbeat. It supplies deterministic logic evidence; IRQ latency, interrupted evaluator registers and EL0 progress need guest evidence.

호스트 대조는 EL1 생산자와 PPI30 소비자가 실제 호출하는 상태 전이를 사용한다. 기한 직전 게시의 단 한 번 반영, 기한과 한 틱 이후 게시 거부, 미게시 취소, 지난 셀·비활성 응답·중복 소비를 구분한다. 지난 생산자가 새 셀의 유효 응답을 지우지 않아야 한다. 하트비트 정지 첫 셀, 독립 예약 우선, 정확히 두 셀째의 감시 만료, 증가 후 기간 초기화를 실제 이유·선택·저장 상태로 대조한다.

## Live evidence prerequisite / 실제 관측의 전제

The present operator instruction set returns a bounded decision even for a self-jump. No supported ordinary rule guarantees a missing publication. A live positive cancellation requires an actual delegated cutoff with no timely reply, its original cell and heartbeat, observed PPI30, trusted fallback/idle return and subsequent reserved product. A live positive watchdog requires observed unchanged progress through the inclusive threshold at an eligible delegated decision. Claim late *publication rejection* only with a witnessed late attempt and cell; claim active interruption only with an IRQ PC on the evaluator's own stack. If a future owner adopts a real workload or failure source that naturally produces these events, freeze that named input, products and original bounds before acquisition. Retain missing observations. This supplement adds no opcode, delay, repeated search or additional guest position. 현재 지원하는 일반 규칙은 자기 점프도 정해진 연료 안에 응답하므로 미게시를 보장하는 입력이 없다. 실제 미게시·감시 만료는 해당 셀과 타이머, 변하지 않은 하트비트, 대체 실행·예약 앱 결과가 함께 관측될 때만 인정한다. 늦은 게시 시도나 해석 중 실제 IRQ는 각 사건의 직접 근거가 필요하다. 이후 운용 주체가 그런 사건을 자연스럽게 만드는 입력을 채택하면 입력과 기준을 먼저 고정한다. 이 보충안에는 새 명령·지연 주입·추가 부팅이 없다.
