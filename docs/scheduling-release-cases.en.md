## POL-003 / #81 ordinary workload acceptance

Select JNOX_PROFILE=batch and JNOX_APP=batch-sjf, batch-srtf or batch-hrrn on virt with no Cargo bench features. Run all three with and without JNOX_BATCH_TRACE=1. The producer submits two older, equal-size jobs and a smaller job delayed by 0.1 architectural-counter seconds. Compare work-begin/end, peer entry and checksum continuation. SJF runs the short job after work ends, SRTF during work, and HRRN runs both older jobs in order before the short one for the declared intervals. All profiles must reject the invalid estimates, overflowing release, foreign/stale completion and full admission, then retain a job that has overrun its single-tick estimate when it submits an equal one-tick peer. Drain all work.

The [batch workload guide](ordinary-batch.en.md) specifies the 15-field diagnostic record, build selectors and QMP replay commands. Confirm the timer interrupts producer code with SRTF, then the short job's completion returns to the identical frame and ELR. Confirm incumbent retention for SJF/HRRN. Trace storage is bounded to 64 events and flushes on entry to the empty idle loop after recording the terminal completion. Reject truncated captures. Scheduling ranks use actual service samples. Compare unexpected ordering to timing and retain failed attempts.
