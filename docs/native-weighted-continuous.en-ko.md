# Continuous weighted native CPU leases / 네이티브 가중 CPU의 연속 퀀텀

[#327](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/327) · CHG-132 · baseline ac3965d4a0865172b73657cb510c3a4932b096a8. [Original #177 acceptance](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/blob/7549e57614221f4e6f71db32b0b369ef390e97f7/docs/scheduling-release-cases.en.md) · [#184 timing investigation](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/184). Allow an operator to give even the last runnable application a real recurring weighted lease while other apps sleep. Ordinary weighted operation without this option preserves uninterrupted solo service. The comparison concerns one native engineering deployment on single-vCPU Cortex-A72 QEMU virt/GICv2.

## Operator interface / 사용자 인터페이스

~~~sh
python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out share --policy weighted --weighted-continuous --weighted-notify closures:36 --quantum-ticks 625000 --elf monitor.elf --weight 1 --arg A --arg share --peer-elf monitor.elf --peer-weight 2 --peer-arg B --peer-arg share
python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out wake --policy weighted --weighted-continuous --weighted-notify wakes:3 --quantum-ticks 625000 --elf monitor.elf --weight 1 --arg A --arg wake --peer-elf monitor.elf --peer-weight 2 --peer-arg B --peer-arg wake
~~~

Continuous mode applies to ordinary weighted ELF slots on virt, at arbitrary supported priorities of service expressed by the existing positive weights, with or without a notification. Use existing ELF/argument limits, unsigned quantum range and process isolation. The optional notification grammar is exactly closures:N or wakes:N (positive decimal u64). Reject duplicates, zero/overflow, unknown modes, incompatible board/policy and notification without continuous. Apply equivalent raw build checks. Include mode, weights, quantum, horizon and ELF hashes/argv in manifest and UART. The example monitor sees argc=3, argv[1]=A or B and argv[2]=share or wake. Ordinary programs can ignore the notification page entirely.

Only when requested map a zeroed shared normal read-only EL0 page at 0x01200000 in each participating address space, PXN/UXN, no writable EL0 alias; its first aligned u64 transitions once from 0 to 1 through an EL1 writable alias. Other bytes stay zero. Count closures on actual IRQ ends of elapsed full quanta (C=R+Q, I≥C); a wake cutting a lease short or an SVC cannot create a full closure. Count wakes on actual timer processing of blocked→ready transitions, once per process per sleep. Idle timer entries alone never count. For simultaneous full expiry/wake both event types are individually attributable. Publish before the next user return at the chosen threshold; latch permanently without counter wrap. No forced exit or forced selection: continue normal scheduling until each application voluntarily exits. The kernel does not inspect application results or identify the monitor by symbol. Capture reads private RAM externally.

단독 실행 중에도 실제 물리 타이머로 퀀텀을 종료한다. 선택적으로 0x01200000의 읽기 전용 첫 단어로 실제 퀀텀 종료 횟수 또는 기상 횟수 도달을 통지한다. 각 앱의 주소 공간·인자·자발적 종료를 유지하며 사설 결과는 외부에서 확인한다.

## Runtime and measurement / 실행·측정

Every selected owner gets a real Q, even when selected again alone. Use the true assembly return-path counter R after selection/restore preparation, immediately before timer setup and ERET; arm physical timer C=min(R+Q, earliest pending absolute wake) using that exact sampled R. A separately sampled Rust dispatch timestamp cannot stand in for this endpoint. Preserve the actual CVAL/CTL, owner, start, sequence, original observed compare and reason, earliest assembly counter I on the next IRQ/SVC vector entry, saved/restored PC/SP, translation root/ASID, frame identity and actual ERET address. Record IAR30/EOI and distinguish idle, full expiry, earlier wake, and SVC. Record any delayed or unexpected IRQ and the full elapsed interval; an early stale IRQ cannot open a new lease or count a closure.

For this mode split accounting at every actual scheduling exception of the historical workload. Valid CLOCK_MONOTONIC read SVC 6 closes and charges the incoming lease at the vector timestamp, performs the read, keeps the caller ready, then reselects by exact weighted vruntime (equal rank chooses lower slot in the frozen original). This can change owner. It never counts as a full IRQ closure. Absolute-sleep SVC 7 similarly charges then blocks on an actual future deadline. Exit SVC 1 charges its final partial service and removes only that caller. Preserve the physical I−R for each positive lease, weighted whole/remainder and monotone runnable floor; a sleeper returns at max(saved vruntime, current floor). Correctly join the producing syscall and its next architectural return, even when another process is selected; preserve caller buffer and eventual return value. Regular syscall validation still applies. Use the actual accepted wake time and frame; no wake arises from a clock read or an inferred boundary. Store diagnostic evidence in bounded memory and expose lost rows. Work continues after notification; retain its tail as separately identified data.

하나의 준비 작업만 있어도 R에서 Q 뒤의 실제 비교기를 설정하고 더 이른 기상 기한이 있으면 그 기한을 사용한다. 원래 모니터의 시각 읽기 SVC도 실제 진입까지 서비스로 정산하고 다시 선택하되 퀀텀 만료로 세지 않는다. 틱 절삭이나 타이머 이전 표본으로 임대 시간을 줄이지 않는다.

## Acceptance / 판정

Use the original 62,500,000 Hz counter, Q=625,000 ticks, H=781,250, G=156,250. Every grant including SVC and post-notification tail has positive measured service ≤H; preceding gap from previous stop to R is ≤G (no fabricated initial gap). A full IRQ closure requires Q≤I−R≤H, actual armed compare and physical IRQ. A wake-shortened IRQ must reach its actual compare and satisfy the same upper bound. Do not charge kernel gaps, cap elapsed charges or discard overruns.

* **Share.** Require 36 real full timer closures with both jobs runnable throughout. At every prefix −H≤2A−B≤2H for cumulative observed service A,B. At 36, T=A+B≥36Q and |A/T−1/3|≤2H/(108Q). Publish at closure 36, retain subsequent voluntary exit tails separately and keep the exact first-36 denominator.
* **Wake.** In each of three B sleeps target=before+40 ms. B receives zero service while blocked; A accumulates at least 3Q and three to four full IRQ closures. Record final partial deadline IRQ separately. After B's actual wake, allow at most one intervening A lease; check floor placement and B's own resumed clock read and progress. Write D for accepted absolute target, I for expiry IRQ sample, W for post-placement wake acceptance, R for B's selected assembly pre-ERET, S for its first resumed clock result. Require D≤I≤W≤R≤S, I−D≤G, W−I≤G, R−W≤H+2G, S−R≤G and S−D≤H+5G in each round. Convert published ns n to inclusive guest ticks [epoch+ceil(nF/10⁹), epoch+ceil((n+1)F/10⁹)−1]; intersect with that producing SVC's entry-through-next-return bracket. Score worst endpoints; empty or crossing bounds fails. Check all three triples, each positive sleep result transition and the monitor's normal zero return from each successful clock/sleep call.

원래 수치·분모·모든 접두 구간을 고정한다. 전체 서비스 상한과 처리 간격, 세 번의 40 ms 수면, 기상별 다섯 구간, 각 앱의 실제 결과·복귀를 각각 판정한다. 통지 후 꼬리 구간은 별도로 보존한다.
