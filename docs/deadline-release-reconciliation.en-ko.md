## Minimal integrated cohort / 최소 통합 실험

Before any boot, produce **one immutable combined binding** for the four following positions. Include main SHA, toolchain, kernel and ordered application ELF bytes/digests, resolved configs, symbol map, exact default-clock QEMU/argv, expected witnesses, raw capture command, scorer revision, file destinations and 120 s cold timeout. Compare each archived dependency (selection, runtime, vectors, build options, layouts, service ABI, decoder) with current source or reproducibly built binaries. Explicitly record whether the loaded executable behavior/config and scoring operands can be carried forward. Reopen surviving raw archives offline; record missing observations as missing. A digest, old exit status or just rerunning the revised parser cannot restore a lost raw observation.

Use the [original operator commands and finite admission rules](native-deadline-recurring.en-ko.md) with one virt Cortex-A72, GICv2, TCG default clock, 128 MiB, F=62,500,000 and Q=6,250,000 ticks (100 ms). First positions in order: EDF plain, EDF external exception trace, LLF plain, LLF external exception trace. Register A=(0,12,1), B=(0,13,4), full reserved cells 1 and 3, cap 6, two independently processed 64-sample service activations. All four use identical app binaries, inputs and private code paths. Keep plain/traced scoring separate. No affinity change, delay injection, calendar tuning or retries. One new four-position cohort closes the prospective binding gap and exercises interference on the integrated main. It cannot replace any prior first. If dependencies or semantics differ for a reused row, declare that row unqualified on main until its **specific original fixture** is rerun once in the affected observation modes. Preserve unchanged linked/refusal/alternate fixtures via their original archives; no blanket repeat.

1. Independently reconstruct epoch, actual PPI30 CVAL/entry and assignment of every observed grid k. Require each operational IRQ in its designated half-open grid cell and zero skipped/duplicate cells for these firsts. For each runnable job recompute chronological FIFO ties, remaining estimate floor, signed slack and absolute deadline under its selected policy; identify EDF A vs LLF B at initial dispatch. Preemption from newly arrived jobs, tie retention/arrival-free crossover, service over estimate, and negative slack have distinct witnesses in the table. The recurring fixture need not exhibit each.
2. Service must publish 89,440 and 617,824, resume with ordinal 1, exit 41. Charge both full reserved cells to interference, including residual genuine idle after the service waits/exits. A must deliver 16 blocks and sum 8,355,840, exit 17. B must deliver 67,108,864 samples, bins 262,144×C(8,k), k=0..8, exit 197. Check actual A/B completion against E+12Q/E+13Q. Equality meets deadline. At least one interrupted regular job per nominal run must show its saved PC/SP, restored identity/mapping and subsequent useful progress. Preserve raw UART, terminal/private RAM, disassembly and event numbering; use external exception/return correspondence only for trace positions.
3. Recompute every half-open Q window at actual handling timestamps using disjoint source/return owners (idle,A,B,service). Include idle→first, service wait/exit→idle, ordinary exits that transfer, and every real IRQ and SVC, even same-frame or terminal rows. Report peak entries, peak returning changes, interval/operands, adjacent change dwell, maximum IRQ lateness, individual completion margins and whole-run totals. Same-owner and nonreturning terminal entries contribute zero changes. The named analytical bound is 2 grid entries + at most 2 reserved wait/exit changes + at most 2 regular exits = 6 changes/Q, contingent on enforced cell and syscall premises. Measured peak 3 in the old cohort is a comparison operand, not a new gate. Never equate the linked bound 4 with native bound 6 or logical one-switch-per-grid with a physical-time frequency bound.
4. Independently apply the frozen runtime classifier and [public replay tool](../tools/replay_native_deadline_service.py); bind actual supply and peak operands rather than acceptance banners. Confirm archived two-mode cap and supply refusals precede operational arm/EL0, altered 2,4 calendar moves service work, and compatibility packages remain distinct. Check malformed and incomplete raw records fail or withhold affected verdict. A missing kernel field must be obtained from independently matched RAM/exception data or left unobserved. Publish first outcomes and counterexamples before any supplemental acquisition.

통합 메인에서 네 최초 위치(EDF 일반·추적, LLF 일반·추적)의 결합 해시·설정·도구·제한시간을 **모두 실행 전** 봉인한다. 두 운영 작업과 독립 서비스 및 셀 1·3을 동일하게 사용해 모든 실제 마감·결과·레지스터 복원·유휴 잔여분을 대조한다. 실제 사건 시간의 모든 반열린 Q 구간에서 진입 수와 소유자 변경 수, 지연과 인접 전환 간격을 각각 계산한다. 기존 최대 관측 3을 승인 기준으로 바꾸지 않는다. 연관 바이너리 또는 의미가 달라진 경우만 해당 원래 실험을 별도로 재수행한다. 누락된 원시 자료와 과거 지연 결합 파일은 과거 기록으로 남긴다.

## Release decision / 출시 결정

Release may decide only the specified virtual deployment after the evidence ledger names every original requirement, its target and its qualified witness, or identifies a remaining hold. No hardware transport or board-level dwell, worst-case interrupt latency, service WCET, blocking, arrival burst or interference allocation follows from these finite completions. For another adopted application/board the deployment owner supplies actual runnable sets, arrival/deadline/estimate and execution envelopes, jitter/blocking/interference ceilings and switching budget. Systems maps operating scenarios and target board; Functional Safety and the designated integration authority own applicable hazard allocations and acceptance. Keep physical target measurements and product approval open where those inputs are absent. [#1](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/1) · [#21](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/21)

출시 담당은 원래 요건마다 타깃·관측·판정을 명시한 뒤 명명된 가상 배포만 결정한다. 실제 제품·보드의 도착/실행 범위·블로킹·지터·간섭 및 전환 예산, 타깃 측정과 권한자 승인이 없으면 해당 범위는 미결로 유지한다.
