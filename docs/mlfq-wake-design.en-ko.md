# Wake-to-receipt engineering profile / 깨움부터 처리 완료까지 (#264)

CPU0 virt, linked Rust, four immutable slots, initially ready in declaration order. Set JNOX_BOARD=virt JNOX_PROFILE=multilevel and JNOX_APP=mlfq-wake or mlfq-wake-refuse. Run both with trace unset and JNOX_MULTILEVEL_TRACE=1. U=floor(CNTFRQ/100) counts. Levels [2,4,6]U, boost P=10U. Reject zero, unsupported cardinality, checked arithmetic failure, or limit below B before launching. The passing limit is B=23U; the second selector changes only the limit to B−1 count.

Slot 0 continuously processes sample blocks until slot 3 has published data. Slots 1–3 process independent streams; consuming a full top allotment rotates each behind its peers at rank 1. On its second turn slot 0 requests another sample and blocks, slot 1 supplies a computed word and wakes it. With nominal charges slots 2 and 3 are ahead at wake; slot 2 consumes the residual to the epoch. Slot 3 precedes slot 0 at reset and receives a top grant. Slot 0 returns from its block, folds the supplied word into a complete block, publishes the receipt and samples the counter; peers consume the receipt and finish their streams. Verify actual timer demotion, lower-rank sleep and successful wake, reset with ready target and an ahead peer, dispatch of that top peer and restoration of the target's saved block frame. Original failures remain evidence.

Origin W is the stop counter on the successful wake SVC (before kernel enqueue, after all waker service charged through W). End E is the task counter immediately after publication. W-to-first-reset owner time <= P+D with a single crossing interval lateness D=U even on SVC or coalescence. Before that boundary yields preserve cumulative service. After reset each of at most three older peers gets at most A+D (A=2U). Yield, block, exit or expiry removes that peer or puts it behind target; subsequent resets preserve FIFO ties. No arrivals, migrations, new head insertion or target withdrawal. Each outgoing interval ending after W must satisfy stop <= due+D. A later boost inside a grant only shortens it. First target dispatch to E consumes at most R=U on that same dispatch (no intervening stop). All non-owner gaps from W through first target start, including processing of wake and final selection, total at most H=2U. Start-to-ERET and exception entry through stop are already owner time. Hence B=P+D+3(A+D)+R+H. Check wall E−W equals gaps plus pre/post-reset and final partial owner service. Finite service progress, delivery, overhead and execution budgets are conditional virtual inputs; no WCET or product claim.

단일 가상 CPU 네 태스크가 선언 순서로 시작한다. 단위는 CNTFRQ/100의 정수부, 등급별 할당량은 [2,4,6]U, 부스트 주기는 누적 소유 시간 10U이다. 0번은 3번의 데이터가 도착할 때까지 블록을 계산하므로 실제 타이머로 강등된다. 다음 차례에 새 샘플을 요청하고 잠든다. 1번은 계산값을 전달하고 깨운다. 2번 실행 중 부스트, 앞선 3번에 최고 등급 서비스, 저장 프레임으로 복귀한 0번의 완전한 블록 계산과 결과 게시, 세 동료의 결과 소비를 확인한다.

성공한 깨움 SVC의 정지 시각 W부터 결과 게시 직후 E까지 측정한다. 깨운 태스크의 W 이전 서비스는 제외한다. 첫 초기화까지 P+D, 이후 앞선 동료 최대 세 개마다 A+D이다. 양보·수면·종료·만료 후 FIFO 선두로 재진입하지 못하며 재초기화도 순서를 유지한다. 병합된 주기에도 첫 경계의 초과 D=U를 검사한다. 모든 해당 경계의 지연을 검사하고 첫 재배정부터 E까지 중단 없는 R=U와 모든 정지~시작 간격 합 H=2U를 더한다. 인터럽트 진입과 복원 비용 중 이미 소유 시간에 들어간 부분을 다시 더하지 않는다. 두 선택자의 한도는 23U와 그보다 한 카운트 작은 값이다. 0·범위 밖·오버플로를 실행 전 거부한다. 명명된 가상 입력의 조건부 판정이며 제품·실기 입력은 #1/#10/#34에 남는다.

## Capture and reconstruction / 원본 수집·재구성

Use the ordinary multilevel build command with each public selector and both trace settings. Run the public boot observer with the matching profile, ELF, a fresh MLFQ_WAKE_CAPTURE_DIR and --qemu tools/mlfq_wake_capture.py. The wrapper freezes RAM, including on incomplete transcripts. Retain the external boot verdict. The same ELF symbol mlfq_wake_witness uses little-endian u64s in both builds. Host arithmetic control: rustc --test tools/mlfq_wake_bound_tests.rs followed by the produced test executable.

Header words 0–4 are state (1 admitted, 2 refused, 4 verified, 5 violation), U, B, limit and row count. Words 5–10 are W, first reset stop, post-reset owner charge until target dispatch, target dispatch start, E, total stop-to-start gaps. Words 11–16 are demotion seen, blocked frame, owner total at wake/reset, elapsed, final partial. Words 17–19 are slot 3 first packet, request flag, supplied word. Words 20–23 and 24–27 contain per-slot packet counts and final running digests before receipt. Words 28–31 contain four receipts, 32 is supplier packet sequence, 33 is the post-reset peer mask, 39 is first violation stage. After 40 words, up to 64 rows of 15 words store outgoing kind/result/slot/start/due/stop, total owner counts, reset flag, FIFO nibbles, selected slot/start, target rank after boundary, outgoing and selected frame addresses, and target rank just before reset (zero in the initial row). IDs start at zero, sentinel four. The terminal row has no dispatch. Traced runs additionally contain the ordinary full-frame journal and limit extension. Check all context returns against frame addresses and saved PCs.

For independent output reconstruction initialize slot i digest to i+1. For packet s=1 through count, for j=0..31 XOR the digest with (rotate_left_64(s,j) XOR j), multiply by 1099511628211 modulo 2^64. Slot 3 publishes its first packet; slot 1 supplies its digest at the recorded supply count. Slot 0 XORs its last digest with the supplied word and processes packet count+1. Each peer likewise XORs its last digest with slot 0's receipt and processes count+1. The endpoint is sampled after writing that receipt. 원본 헤더에는 입력·깨움·부스트·서비스·간격·각 계산 결과를, 각 행에는 슬롯·시각·누적량·큐·프레임을 보존한다. 원본에서 직접 재계산하고 실패한 최초 실행도 보존한다.

A reset at the wake boundary costs zero owner time after W. Charge the following gap in full. Multiple epoch crossings after W violate the lateness input D<P and fail. A refusal applies to the analytic limit regardless of actual run duration. 깨움과 초기화가 같은 경계이면 W 이후 초기화까지 소유 시간은 0이며 다음 간격은 모두 포함한다. W 이후 여러 주기 동시 경과는 가정 위반이다. 거부는 실측값과 독립적인 한도 판정이다.
