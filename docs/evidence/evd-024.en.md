Across 12 slots the leaf sequence was 1/2/2/3/3/3 twice. The readiness mask excluded funded child 0. When parent 0 exhausted its budget, ready leaf 2 still had four leaf slots and its child had two. Refill restored child 1 at slot 6. The verifier matched four SVC waits, the idle IRQ, 12 leaf IRQs and return PCs. Cookie and IRQ-ID checks are attested by the guest PASS marker.
