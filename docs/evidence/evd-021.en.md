# EVD-021 · Ticket selection and actual stride service

[한국어](evd-021.md) · [Acceptance contract](../m4-proportional.en.md) · [#54](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/54)

The first verifier reproduced all 80 original decisions, then identified a 40 Hz stride tick against the specified 25 Hz and an IRQ path that charged the programmed grant even when a handler ran late. None of the initial 80 intervals exceeded its expected whole-tick charge. That run exposed the discrepancy through examination of the frozen image. [Initial observation and findings](evd-021-initial.json)

A different verifier cold-booted the corrected image with an additional delayed IRQ. The independent replay checked all 81 rows, 75 IRQs, six SVCs and the associated frame returns. Across 32 lottery selections for each seed, A/B/C won 10/8/14 and 5/11/16 times. In stride, service was 4/8/0 before C joined and 2/4/8 after admission at the incumbent floor. The final task held delivery of a two-tick grant until three ticks had elapsed. PPI30 interrupted PC `0x40201310`. The handler measured 7,619,345 counter counts at 2,500,000 counts per stride tick, charged all three whole ticks and advanced the weight-one pass by 2,162,160. [Independent replay, UART, exceptions and 81 rows](evd-021.json)

The 2026-11-24 final source passed 93 Rust tests, 19 Python tests and 11 additional sequential virtual boots. Product interference and timing remain open.
