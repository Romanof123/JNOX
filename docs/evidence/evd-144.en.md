# EVD-144 · EDF and sampled LLF deadlines with reserved service

CHG-144 / POL-005 / [#350](../deadline-release-reconciliation.en-ko.md)


On 9f4ce082600fe73e5ee3e8b98ae826d9d52a4ed3, EDF and sampled LLF each completed a plain and an externally traced first boot. One Cortex-A72 QEMU virt/GICv2 CPU ran at the default 62.5 MHz with Q=6,250,000 ticks and service cells 1 and 3 reserved. EDF selected A first and LLF selected B. At A/B deadlines E+12Q/E+13Q, 10/11 available cells covered demand of 1/5 cells. Both entire reserved cells (12,500,000 ticks) counted as interference despite early wait and exit.

Across all four boots, private mappings supplied A’s 16 blocks and sum 8,355,840, B’s 64 MiB and nine bins of 262,144×C(8,k), and the service’s two 64-sample sums of 89,440/617,824. Exits were 17/197/41. B resumed at PC 0x40003c and SP 0x1010fa0. Each boot recorded nine entries and eight changes, peaking at 3/3 per sliding Q. The admitted bound of six allows two grid entries, two reserved returns and two regular exits. Maximum measured PPI30 lag was 21,938 ticks. Operands at the two linked CVAL writers and the journal reconstructed the programmed compare. There was no independent at-entry CVAL readback. Equality at deadline was an offline control.

EVD-095 retains the SVC overrun, B not running and missing original raw logs. EVD-117 retains its missed and clock-helper cases, and EVD-121 its large-input deadline misses. EVD-100/104 have separate linked interference bounds. Compared with EVD-128, all rebuilt whole ELF hashes differed. B’s allocated sections matched, while added SDK wrappers enlarged and relocated A and service .text. Current bytes were checked for products, frames and deadlines. Host scheduling passed 194 cases and supply passed 12. This verification reran existing implementations. Product workloads, arrivals, blocking, jitter and safety allocations require owner approval, and physical WCET requires separate measurement. Release disposition is pending.
