# EVD-140 · Matched idle-refill delivery observations

CHG-140 / IMP-140 / POL-016 / [#343](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/343) / [#344](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/344)

[Observation design / 관측 설계](../native-idle-delivery-time.en-ko.md)

The first guest pair on 31a4185 used identical archived kernel and application ELFs and the default 62.5 MHz clock. Control and instrumented boots recorded 29 and 30 refills. Neither had late or invalid refills or lost rows. Maximum lags were 22,179 and 16,244 ticks against the original 1,953,125-tick limit. Private RAM verified reporter progress/result 3/7, nine histogram bins and energy 5,864,240,971,776. The apps exited 7/70/179.

The 120 callbacks matched four sites for each of 30 instrumented refills. The host sampler retained 1,530 observations with zero skips or drops. Calibration waits of 74,295,378 and 74,195,991 ns came from two disposable children on CPU0. The vCPU was sampled separately. No emitter-timestamped IRQ assertion or new late refill was available for causal attribution, and the pair establishes no observer-cost bound.

The first CLI exited 2 when it could not identify the actual Rust symbol. A separate replay supplied the full symbol from the retained ELF, and an independent page-table and exception replay verified the results. The stock CLI first retains its failure. The original 4,201,556-, 6,487,068- and 9,989,268-tick misses remain unattributed under #339. POL-016 remains open.
