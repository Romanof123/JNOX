# EVD-142 · HI transitions and job generations in native mixed-criticality applications

CHG-142 / IMP-142 / POL-015 / [#347](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/347) / [#348](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/348)


In the first SDK preflight all four C application builds exited 1 before linking. The corrected SDK produced four AArch64 ELFs and the two- and four-slot packages. The guest kernel tested was f2b2cd724ff8e3d8e7e403c5cc6a6e819f5f4ff0. The accepted main used for this documentation integration is 3373dcc6ce8230cc65240fe78f6500fb2798f6b6.

[First SDK preflight / 최초 SDK 점검](native-amc-first-preflight.en-ko.md) · [Corrected binaries / 수정 바이너리](native-amc-corrected-preflight.en-ko.md)

All 12 first boots (six configurations, plain and traced) on one default-clock 62.5 MHz Cortex-A72 QEMU virt/GICv2 CPU reached terminal records with zero interval or lifecycle drops. The original reader scored eight observations met, two incomplete and two failed. In normal-hi and staggered traced, HI1/g0 crossed on a CPU-clock SVC sampled 125 and 1,145 ticks past CVAL respectively, with zero PPI30s for that HI generation. Private application samples observed the committed HI transition before the next boundary. The timer-only ordering requirement remains unmet in those two firsts. Over-hi traced recorded a genuine PPI30 transition. Normal-hi produced eight samples summing to 36 in fresh LO0/g1 and the surviving job completed. Over-hi also verified fresh-generation output and HI budget exhaustion.

In overlap plain and traced, LO0/g0 exited 2,875,851 and 2,665,910 ticks past its intentional deadline respectively. Both recorded a deadline fault and refused the overlapping release, then charged the original generation to its actual exit with eight samples, sum 36 and status 0. The original scorer failed both late exits. Separate offline analysis of the same firsts scored ten met and two incomplete, but falsely accepted two controls moving fault and refusal 25 ms past the real decision. The further reader change at bba5355 awaits independent acceptance.

Physical timing and authority over product workloads, budgets, deadlines and risk remain separate. The open work is tracked under #353 and POL-015.
