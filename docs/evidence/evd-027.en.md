# EVD-027 · Priority resume and RR residual service

[한국어](evd-027.md) · [Acceptance contract](../m1-priority-resume.en.md) · [#63](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/63)

Five prelaunch invocations failed during setup. The sixth invocation produced the first guest. Its 21 rows showed A/A/HIGH/A/B/A/idle under preemptive FIFO and RR, and A/A/A/HIGH/B/A/idle under deferred FIFO. The verifier matched seven IRQs and eleven SVCs. RR charged 1,662,259 and 1,650,670 counts, leaving 2,937,071 of the original 6,250,000 counts. Expiry was sampled 102,071 counts after its programmed deadline, within the 781,250-count limit.

The published journal schema was incomplete at the time of this verdict. The verifier checked the contract, trace, ELF and audit before examining source. This one virtual boot covers the priority-resume profile. Legacy FIFO/RR regressions and physical timing require separate evidence.

## Independent replay using the published journal schema

A separate cold boot used the same ELF and the revised observer, which invoked the capture script directly with Python. The independent verifier decoded all 18 fields in each of 21 rows and matched 36 chronological exception and return events. In this run A resumed with 2,875,451 counter counts of RR service remaining. The expiry IRQ arrived 137,104 counts after its programmed deadline, within the 781,250-count tolerance.
