# EVD-042 v2 · FS-M3-01 runtime replay after startup and boundary changes

[한국어](evd-042-v2.md) · [EVD-042](evd-042.en.md) · [#94](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/94)

**PARTIAL.** Fourteen of 23 complete primary cases qualified on single-core Cortex-A72 QEMU 6.2.0. The ten diagnostics used the same immutable ELFs with separately selected clock modes. Each initial launch with an implicit NIC failed before guest startup because QEMU lacked efi-virtio.rom. The substantive launches disabled the unused NIC. The first RM healthy handoff guest had a truncated host read, no terminal marker and no recorded exit status. Its separate cold retry qualified. Both attempts are archived.

| Primary workload | Observed result |
|---|---|
| Default clock, both policies, healthy/loss/late/demand (8) | All inhibited before enable. Phase-zero IRQ latency was 111,771–123,682 ticks against 31,250 allowed. Each later skipped a phase. Attribution to host scheduling or logging remains unresolved. |
| Deterministic healthy/loss handoffs (4) | Both policies qualified. Issues at 190,111 and 815,111 ticks preceded the command at 1,127,611. RM loss enabled readback at 1,252,671–1,252,672 and transferred at 1,252,676. Both loss runs closed at 2,065,123, latched at 3,440,142 and observed OFF at 3,440,170. Conservative intervals were 22.000304/0.000448/22.000752 ms against 25/5/30 ms limits. |
| Deterministic worst phase (1) | Issue, accepted delivery and closure occurred at 1,408,830 ticks. Phase 6 completed at 2,658,829, one tick short of 20 ms after issue. Phase 7 completed at 2,971,329, exactly 312,500 ticks later. Detection/inhibition/total upper intervals were 1,562,499/5/1,562,504 ticks. |
| Deterministic tie (1) | Expiry and fourth issue were sampled at 2,658,830. The ingress store followed the latch in instruction order, but its bracket ended at 2,658,831. Rejection occurred at 2,658,837 and OFF readback at 2,658,840. Same-tick ingress remains unqualified. |
| Other deterministic RM scripts (9) | Starting, inhibited and terminal device states matched expectations. Only recovery re-enabled after closure and latch. |

Four default-clock handoff diagnostics failed before transfer. Under deterministic instruction counting both ordinary healthy runs passed. The late controls isolated one late phase-12 IRQ (RM/DM 39,063/39,064 ticks) without excess service. The demand controls isolated 14 excessive activations per run (peak 19,548 versus 15,625 ticks) with all IRQs inside the envelope. All four negative controls observed a timing latch and a 1→0 register transition.

The standalone CLI raised IndexError on 12 complete traces after a skipped phase. It also falsely rejected four deterministic negatives by counting post-latch issues as required deliveries. The independent verdict uses the raw event stream. The virtual PL061 readback and local sender supply no evidence of electrical response, safety IPC transport, board timing or general analytical admission.
