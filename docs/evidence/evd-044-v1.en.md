# EVD-044 v1 · Temporal partitioning with ordinary Rust workers

[EVD-044](evd-044.en.md) · [한국어](evd-044-v1.md)

**PARTIAL. Both first ordinary guests failed the 1 ms timing criterion.** P exceeded it at 9 of 10 edges and Q at 11 of 12. A runnable B window was skipped in Q. The independent replay checked exception PCs, private-stack continuity, worker checksums and programmed compare readbacks. The unchanged assembly fixture also emitted PART FAIL. The cause remains open. The six synthetic cases supply host controls only. Issue #99 and the #21 release matrix remain open.

At 62.5 MHz the 1 ms limit was 62,500 ticks from the nominal boundary to the first worker observation. P’s peak was 369,103 ticks (5.905648 ms), Q’s was 3,575,899 ticks (57.214384 ms). P’s first failed edge went from nominal 6,835,696 through IRQ entry 6,978,098, selection 6,985,288 and return sample 6,988,312 to observation 7,010,107. Q’s corresponding samples were 4,601,418 / 4,743,182 / 4,748,790 / 4,751,155 / 4,771,039. The overruns at these first failed edges were observable even though the programmed compare readbacks equaled the nominal deadlines.

B became eligible at counter 9,960,696 in P and 4,601,418 in Q. Its first actual selections were 19,674,180 and 14,092,803. P skipped no whole window. Q skipped its eligible B window from tick 5 to 6 at generation 4. The trace subsequently recorded same-owner returns. The independent replay matched outgoing ELRs and exception returns with the linked worker code, checked guest-recorded SPs against the three aligned 16 KiB stack ranges, and replayed saved state and accumulator checksums.

Return-to-first-sample and last-sample-to-IRQ intervals remain uncertain. The host CLI correctly rejected both late guest UARTs before emitting accounting. All six synthetic healthy/near/long controls and four unit tests passed. The CLI also accepted a corrupted worker value and an initial return PC of 1. Its first-selection field can denote idle at B release, not B dispatch (synthetic P: release 103000, reported 103002, actual B 106002). Independent replay checked these data against the original ELF disassembly and exception trace.

The separate assembly fixture emitted PART FAIL after A FIRST. Its first IRQ interrupted part_a_begin at ELR `0x40201278`, with no second exception return before timeout. The first trace lacks counter and predicate evidence to explain this failure. Neither default-clock run exercises prescribed masking or controlled delayed notification. Physical isolation, board timing and product release remain outside these captures.
