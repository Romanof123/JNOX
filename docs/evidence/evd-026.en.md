[한국어](evd-026.md) · [Acceptance contract](../m4-bounded-extension.en.md) · [#59](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/59)

One cold boot recorded nine slots. Task 2 received the accepted proposal after two evaluation steps. The UART recorded mask, stale-proposal, fuel and watchdog fallback, an empty idle slot and three reserved slots. The journal recorded PPI30 for all nine service interrupts and six evaluator interrupts. The generic QEMU IRQ trace corroborates the PCs. The interrupt IDs come from the guest journal, which carries no separate source-control field.

The evidence covers this virtual boot. Physical-board timing and product functional safety require separate evidence. ELF SHA-256 `2c20aa2f12ae6083bfcbd29828522118cf3e790024c69981d4c0810a9bb1d29d`.
