# EVD-146 · Partitioned native processes on two Pi 4 model cores

CHG-146 / IMP-146 / POL-013 / [#355](../pi4-native-smp-applications.en-ko.md) / [#356](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/356)


The initial pair, remote and swapped package builds failed on 0297172, preventing all five planned SMP boots. On revised source 5d43712 the same three builds failed in assembly. The single-core legacy FIFO controls retain their separate results.

[Packaging firsts / 최초 패키징](pi4-native-smp-first-packaging.en-ko.md) · [Assembly firsts / 최초 어셈블](pi4-native-smp-first-assembly.en-ko.md)

On 60475a0ba7c2b1092305043a65d1f6182625a8ed, stock raspi4b QEMU 11.1.3 completed five first SMP boots plus a separate FIFO control. C/Rust prime counts were 78,498/70,435 and exits 12/77 in every position. Their common result VA mapped to distinct physical pages and ASIDs. A ran on CPU0 and B on CPU1 in the plain and traced pair. Swapping masks moved A to CPU1 and B to CPU0. Productive-loop PPI counts for A/B were 8/16 in both pair boots, 8/17 and 8/18 in remote plain/traced, and 8/16 in swapped. Persisted frames, service, stack guards and stop rendezvous matched with zero journal loss.

In the remote cases CPU0 targeted CPU1 with SGI1, interrupting B for the optional third app C, which produced 32 and exited 32. Remote plain established reception after send, C’s first clock read before cell 2 and positive B progress during C’s sleep. Remote traced observed the SGI handoff and sleep lifecycle, but its conservative receive lower bound preceded the send by 421 ticks. The strict interleaved-progress overlap criterion remained unresolved in all five SMP firsts (reader exit 2). Only the legacy FIFO control scored 0. The five original prime runs did not exercise nonzero TLS. Physical-board execution, host parallelism and product interference remain unmeasured. The original #47 short-loop requirement and full POL-013 qualification remain open.

## 2026-11-25 · Separate ABI workload first boots

After the initial SIMD assembly build failed, 2423f0a assembled the SIMD routine separately. One plain and one traced boot used the same image. Each CPU completed 33,554,432 iterations per boot. Scalar and SIMD sums matched at 1,125,899,906,842,624 for odd inputs on CPU0 and 1,125,899,940,397,056 for even inputs on CPU1, with exits 12 and 77. The processes used distinct ASIDs and private writable TLS backing, with TPIDR_EL0 values 0x4020a0 and 0x4020a8. At all 12 real PPI30 boundaries (three per core per boot), all 816 bytes saved from the EL0 loop matched the corresponding prepared return frame. The captures also recorded x19, q16 and FP control state.

In the plain boot, same-owner returns, subsequent EL0 entries on each core and increasing private work confirmed resumption. In the traced boot, unlabeled ERET packets at the same virtual PC left individual returns ambiguous by core. Both readers exited 2 because strict overlap remained unverified after clock-offset correction. The five original boots, #47 and physical-board qualification retain their separate verdicts.
