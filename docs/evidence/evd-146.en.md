# EVD-146 · Partitioned native processes on two Pi 4 model cores

CHG-146 / IMP-146 / POL-013 / [#355](../pi4-native-smp-applications.en-ko.md) / [#356](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/356)


The initial pair, remote and swapped package builds failed on 0297172, preventing all five planned SMP boots. On revised source 5d43712 the same three builds failed in assembly. The single-core legacy FIFO controls retain their separate results.

[Packaging firsts / 최초 패키징](pi4-native-smp-first-packaging.en-ko.md) · [Assembly firsts / 최초 어셈블](pi4-native-smp-first-assembly.en-ko.md)

On 60475a0ba7c2b1092305043a65d1f6182625a8ed, stock raspi4b QEMU 11.1.3 completed five first SMP boots plus a separate FIFO control. C/Rust prime counts were 78,498/70,435 and exits 12/77 in every position. Their common result VA mapped to distinct physical pages and ASIDs. A ran on CPU0 and B on CPU1 in the plain and traced pair. Swapping masks moved A to CPU1 and B to CPU0. Productive-loop PPI counts for A/B were 8/16 in both pair boots, 8/17 and 8/18 in remote plain/traced, and 8/16 in swapped. Persisted frames, service, stack guards and stop rendezvous matched with zero journal loss.

In the remote cases CPU0 targeted CPU1 with SGI1, interrupting B for the optional third app C, which produced 32 and exited 32. Remote plain established reception after send, C’s first clock read before cell 2 and positive B progress during C’s sleep. Remote traced observed the SGI handoff and sleep lifecycle, but its conservative receive lower bound preceded the send by 421 ticks. The strict interleaved-progress overlap criterion remained unresolved in all five SMP firsts (reader exit 2). Only the legacy FIFO control scored 0. Nonzero TLS, physical-board execution, host parallelism and product interference remain unmeasured. The original #47 short-loop requirement and full POL-013 qualification remain open.
