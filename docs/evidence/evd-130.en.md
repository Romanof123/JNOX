On original c3c89fd, all 50 edges across P/Q plain and traced met 1 ms (peak 46,087 ticks). An offline reader syntax fix replayed the original captures. The selector used syscall entry time, leaving selection across a boundary during service unverified.

Corrected source 213a8efa1d3dfa72eadc3ecb85fa4924b60ca3b8 selects at the post-operation counter. On one Cortex-A72 virt CPU with the default clock, four fresh P/Q plain and traced boots qualified 50/50 edges. Time from scheduled boundary to first actual EL0 worker sample peaked at 47,016 ticks and to EL1 idle-loop observation at 24,344 ticks (62.5 MHz, limit 62,500 ticks). Consecutive B windows across Q wrap each showed fresh samples and productive work. In the separate P sleep control the tail of blocked B’s window stayed idle and A continued in its own window when B became ready.

In all five boots per candidate, page-table walks verified the private energy sum N × 5,559,680 and histogram N × [1,8,28,56,70,56,28,8,1] using each worker’s own completed block count N. C and Rust exited 41 and 42. No edges were skipped, no journal rows were lost and neither application overflowed its samples. In a separate corrected guest with alternating 1 ms windows, one of 440 clock_gettime calls crossed an edge during A’s syscall and returned to B. Both workers completed 220 batches, produced checksum 229,833,914 and exited 23. This supplement was scored separately from healthy timing.
