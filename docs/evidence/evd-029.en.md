# EVD-029 · Hard CBS blocking and wakeup

[한국어](evd-029.md) · [Acceptance contract](../m2-cbs-wake.en.md) · [#65](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/65)

The first invocation stopped before boot because the launcher could not resolve the ELF path. The first actual guest then reached slots 0–24 before the five-second UART deadline expired. Independent replay matched the captured rows and exceptions. Slots 25–33 and the final UART marker were missing, so the guest run failed. [Prelaunch record](evd-029-initial.json) · [First guest, 25/34 rows](evd-029-partial.json)

The revised host observer allowed 15 seconds. One cold boot of the same ELF completed all 34 slots. Replay matched every grant and 78 exception/return PC events. A blocked by SVC at slots 0, 4, 8, 14 and 22 and resumed at 4, 8, 14, 22 and 23. Neighbor B received nine grants at slots 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29 and 33. The other 17 slots were idle. This single-vCPU QEMU run establishes the finite grant sequence. Physical interrupt latency and product interference require board measurements. [Accepted guest and replay](evd-029.json)
