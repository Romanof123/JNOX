# EVD-007 · Live RM and DM dispatch of repeated releases

[한국어](evd-007.md) · English

**RM ran A first and DM ran B first on identical inputs.** On 2026-11-24 a fresh verifier recorded 13 UART lines, 111 bytes, and observer exit 0 on the first boot of a single Cortex-A72 guest before inspecting instructions or exception logs. Both policies used equal static priorities and enqueued B first. A’s period and relative deadline were 2 and 2 ticks. B’s were 3 and 1. [Public acceptance contract](../m1-periodic.en.md) · [Source candidate](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/commit/62c7fa7ba7c2038a271b141ec24ae5fb924534d8)

| Policy | Tick 0 | Tick 1 | Tick 2 | Tick 3 | Tick 4 | Tick 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RM | A, B | Idle | A | B | A | Idle |
| DM | B, A | Idle | A | B | A | Timer stop |

The verifier identified five job entries per epoch, ten `SVC #1` completions and twelve physical timer IRQs. The jobs used private stacks. The controller returned to the original interrupted instruction `0x40201260` on all eleven nonterminal ticks, after the jobs completed or immediately on an idle tick. The host observer rejected missing releases, reversed tick-zero order and trailing bytes. [Complete trace, UART and observer verdict](evd-007.json)

The ELF SHA-256 is `f2964256be663342b7e92b9dcd7208399bcf9802e9b5a9b258a7edfd6d2c9df6`. A WSL restart cleared the temporary files. The verifier recovered the 4,282-byte exception log and 677-byte runner output from contemporaneous tool output and extracted UART from the recorded bytes literal. One guest launch took place.

Integration also booted two combined configurations in sequence. Periodic plus RR produced the periodic transcript. Periodic plus CBS produced the CBS transcript. The five earlier configurations and the all-features build passed release compile checks. [Combined profile results](evd-007-combinations.json)
