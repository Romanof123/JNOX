# EVD-136 · Native thread priority protocols and mutex handoff

CHG-136 / IMP-136 / POL-014 / [#335](../native-thread-priority-protocols.en-ko.md) / [#336](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/336)

`383f1d40b10798af89d5f3ddf44c456accb96b0b`

On single-CPU Cortex-A72 QEMU virt, 12 priority positions using the same C/Rust ELF and three compatibility positions exited 0 on their first boot. Each priority case reached stage 3 with zero lost rows. Terminal RAM supplied block counts, sums, sums of squares and joined result addresses. All nine traced EL0 return-PC sequences matched. Saved SP/TLS, generation and scheduler state identified the role.

In the chain, HIGH waiting for X raised LOW and BASE to rank 4. BASE resumed the reduction at 0x400044 ahead of ready MEDIUM. Y passed directly to LOW, then X to HIGH. In the timeout case BASE was asleep. HIGH returned ETIMEDOUT (110), while LOW still donated through Y and the two upstream ranks fell to 2. Immediate ceiling changed 1→2→4→2→1. Ranked handoff, both equal-rank FIFO arrival orders and cycle refusal (EDEADLK 35) were observed.

After HIGH interrupted LOW under RR, LOW resumed with exactly 380,969 of 625,000 ticks remaining. Under FIFO, equal-rank MEDIUM ran at LOW exit. Two-thread cooperative and RR controls and the singleton preserved their legacy results. The public packager and raw build each rejected nine invalid inputs (18 rejections). Donation-only RR residual changes, exact hardware-tick coincidence, generation wrap and exhaustive exceptional paths remain unobserved in these guest boots. Product-specific ceilings and physical M3 maximum blocking remain open.

A separate nine-test run on 9aef21d5 observed ETIMEDOUT (110) once at D=150 before unlock or dispatch. At D−1=149, unlock transferred ownership and completion 0 before dispatch. Non-drained controls preserved 0/110/110 at D−1/D/D+1 across later transitions. In the 100-tick RR case, donation left 70 ticks after 30 consumed and withdrawal left 50 after a further 20. These fixed-counter host observations establish the policy decisions. Equal hardware-tick contention and donation-only RR residuals remain unobserved in the guest.
