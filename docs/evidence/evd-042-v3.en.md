# EVD-042 v3 · FS-M3-01 exact boundaries and scorer recovery

[한국어](evd-042-v3.md) · [EVD-042](evd-042.en.md) · [#109](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/109)

**PARTIAL.** Fourteen of 23 primary configurations qualified on their first cold attempt. A fresh retry after a host read error raised the total to 15 of 23. Both exact boundaries qualified under deterministic instruction counting. All eight default-clock ordinary cases remained unqualified. The ten diagnostics are separate.

The independent reviewer matched all 23 ELF digests to the build index. The 23 primary and 10 diagnostic configurations used 34 guest attempts on QEMU 6.2.0, one Cortex-A72, GICv2, TCG and 128 MiB. A prelaunch configuration error started no guest. The first DM handoff-loss host read aborted with errno 61. Its incomplete capture and reconstructed argv are retained. Its runner did not record start time, end time or process status.

| Workload | Observed outcome |
|---|---|
| Primary default-clock healthy, loss, late, demand under RM/DM | 0/8 qualified. Shortened or mistimed phase histories and device trajectories are retained. |
| Primary deterministic ordinary handoff healthy/loss under RM/DM | 3/4 on first captures, 4/4 including the cold retry. |
| Primary deterministic RM state and boundary scripts | 11/11 qualified, including worst and tie. |
| Diagnostic deterministic ordinary | Healthy 2/2. Late/demand 4/4. Each negative control accepted pre-latch issues, rejected subsequent issues in order, observed one falling register transition and ended inhibited. |
| Diagnostic default-clock handoff | 0/4 qualified. |

At 62.5 MHz the third RM tie permit was published and delivered at tick 1,408,831. Exactly 1,250,000 ticks later, the terminal decision, latch bracket, fourth issue and ingress bracket all read 2,658,831. Linked instructions at 0x40200638–0x4020066c place the latch store before the separate fourth issue and publish store. The receiver rejected the fourth permit at 2,658,838. The PL061 read observed OFF at 2,658,841 and again at 2,658,844. In the worst case, the check completed at age 1,249,999 ticks and the next decision followed exactly 312,500 ticks later.

The scorer returned structured results on the tested absent, corrupt and truncated captures, including the partial host read. Three upstream regression tests passed. Eight altered deterministic negative-control inputs were rejected. These host checks preserve the distinction between pre-latch deliveries and post-latch rejections.

The device observation is an in-guest read of the emulated PL061. Neither policy has full ordinary qualification. External electrical response, safety IPC transport, product timing and worst-case interference remain outside the evidence. The cause of default-clock failures remains undetermined pending the separate #109 cold-start experiments.
