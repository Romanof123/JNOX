# EVD-015 · A live laxity crossover on an anchored sampling grid

[한국어](evd-015.md)

EDF started A and LLF started B for the same initial jobs. LLF retained B at the tick-5 tie. At 6 A displaced B without a new arrival, then returned to B’s saved instruction. At 7 C arrived with laxity −2 and displaced B, which again resumed at its interrupted instruction after C completed. EDF also let an earlier-deadline C interrupt A.

The original observer exited 0. Its instrumentation stopped the vCPUs before forwarding the final UART bytes, leaving the live 0.1-second quiet window inconclusive. A second fresh verifier cold-booted the identical ELF. That guest produced 346 bytes, passed the quiet window, and was still running when the observer requested shutdown, as confirmed by QMP. Its eight IRQs and six completion SVCs matched the linked ELF. All eight captured IRQ observations fell inside their absolute windows on a 62.5 MHz guest counter with 6,250,000-count periods. [First](evd-015-first.json) · [UART / QMP / IRQ](evd-015.json)

A separate host model compared 11,520 schedules and all 48 host tests passed. Four single and combined kernel regression configurations also booted successfully. Service estimates and completion timestamps are workload inputs. Schedulability for other workloads and worst-case physical latency require further evidence. [QEMU](evd-015-regressions.json)
