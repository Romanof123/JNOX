The adopted weighted-analytics deployment on one QEMU virt/GICv2 Cortex-A72 used a 62.5 MHz default counter. The 1:2 pair received 7,582,557 and 15,139,512 ticks in 36/36 grants. The original swap retained 12/36 grants (5,042,191 and 2,516,401 ticks). The original triple retained 60/60 (6,365,682, 12,674,716 and 19,023,433 ticks). DrvFS ENODATA prevented verification of terminal products and exits for those two firsts.

Separate ext4 cold boots completed swap 36/36 (15,147,006 and 7,559,210 ticks) and triple 60/60 (6,296,682, 12,579,453 and 18,869,364 ticks), with private products and exits verified and zero drops. In the three 40 ms waits, peer service was 2,471,008, 2,507,666 and 2,506,069 ticks; the reporter incurred zero blocked charge. The distant sleep returned EOVERFLOW (75), the following valid sleep returned 0, and the two applications exited 61 and 237.

All five variants have complete evidence across the five first launches and two supplements. Admission and service were verified for this named virtual deployment. Physical targets and other product configurations retain their own release gates.
