# EVD-055 · Seeded lottery dispatch and measured stride service for ordinary Rust clients

[CHG-057 / IMP-055](../ordinary-proportional-plan.md) · [#136](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/136) · [#54](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/54) · [#79](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/79) · [JSON](evd-055.json) · [ZIP](evd-055-first-three.zip)

All three first cold boots completed on a single Cortex-A72 QEMU TCG CPU with the default clock. A/B/C had weights 1/2/4. Service sums whole observed residency ticks per owner. At 62.5 MHz, a lottery tick is 625,000 counter counts (10 ms) and a stride tick is 2,500,000 counts (40 ms).

| lottery1 | 10/8/14 | 10/8/14 | 33/0/33 | 394 |
| lottery2 | 5/11/16 | 5/11/16 | 33/0/33 | 394 |
| stride | 6/6/4 | 6/12/8 | 11/6/17 | 197 |

Starting from cold seeds 1 and 2, independent replay regenerated the scheduler’s persistent SplitMix64 state and raw words at each eligible dispatch, then checked ticket, half-open interval and actual owner. Of seven tickets, 0 selected A, 1–2 selected B and 3–6 selected C. Each observed selection consumed one word. The initial empty-ready dispatch returned to a real idle frame. RNG continuity across idle was inferred from the subsequent state. The counts describe these finite seeded windows.

Stride admitted C after eight A/B grants. Row 11 recorded incumbent passes 720,720/720,720 and the ready mask changing from 3 to 7. C joined at the incumbent floor. The first and second eight-grant sequences were ABABABAB and ABCCABCC, with incremental service 4/8/0 and 2/4/8. A yielded by SVC after one whole tick; B and C underwent two-tick timer preemptions. External exception replay matched the ordered ERET, IRQ and SVC transfers and PCs, including the initial idle return, to linked clients and saved continuations.

All five first-failure fields were zero in each guest. Progress, stack witnesses, counter samples and GIC values are guest records. External exception tracing corroborates transfer order and PC, without independently measuring instruction-level CPU service or the restore tail. The lottery-miss and stride-delay diagnostics were unexecuted. The historical four-IRQ failure under #79 remains unattributed. Controlled late charging, physical-board interference and product timing guarantees remain open.
