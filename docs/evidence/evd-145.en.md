# EVD-145 · AMC clock-page readings and HI budget timer crossings

CHG-145 / IMP-145 / POL-015 / [#353](../native-amc-clock-pair.en-ko.md) / [#354](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/354)


All six single-CPU QEMU first boots on 896518d8edcfc850dc16361dcde2d1edd87c0d4c reached terminal records with zero interval, lifecycle or evidence drops. Page-enabled normal-hi plain/traced and staggered plain/traced each crossed on a genuine HI1 PPI30. Budget boundary b to the first private application observation x measured 8.535760, 7.518816, 7.127168 and 7.144624 ms respectively. All met the frozen bounds with zero clock SVCs. The separate page-disabled normal-hi trace used 995 fallback pairs and 1,990 clock SVCs, with b→x of 18.683712 ms.

All 663 C/Rust compatibility pairs fell inside their individual CPU and monotonic brackets and exact publication intervals. Row 909 preempted low/g0 for high/g0 at the return gate. After high exited, its original low frame resumed. Fresh low/g1 began with zero settled service and produced eight samples summing to 36. The original public reader required an IRQ-triggered handoff and failed this control (five of six met). Independent verification of the corrected reader at dbc35ace is pending.

The earlier syscall-based #348 normal-hi and staggered traced firsts lacked their required generation-specific PPI crossings. Those historical verdicts stand separately from this clock-page cohort. Physical-board timing, M5, product-risk acceptance and the POL-015 family release remain open.
