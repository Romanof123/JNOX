A 4,000-level nested input returned structured unknown/2 under both policies. A refusal means the supplied assumptions and budget bounds did not prove every limit. An unknown result identifies invalid or unsupported input or an inconclusive calculation.

For the hypothetical example both policies gave phase A/B completion bounds of 185/285 µs, sender completion of 695 µs, and maximum decision gap and control-job finish of 2,785 µs, including uncertainty. In the sender boundary cases, 9,999 and 10,000 µs were admitted conditionally and 10,001 µs was refused. These are calculated bounds.

The analysis assumes one initially idle core, absolute phase targets with no early starts, a single worker continuing into phase B, complete service accounting and independent finite IRQ interference bounds including carry-in. No measured execution budgets, runtime ELF, guest traces, physical board, device state or product-safety validation were part of either host review.
