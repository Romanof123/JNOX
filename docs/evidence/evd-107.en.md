The public MG4 reader scored the twelve first boots on frozen PR #281 source `84214dd36094b4b3b489ab0232e9aa2f4774260b`. All six short positions reached early terminal code `0x1d` (0/6). Their captures establish neither handoff consumption nor terminal RAM phase. All six stream positions qualified. Each sealed trace records consumption of the opening and reverse handoffs, the selected frame, and subsequent worker execution. In both global positions M resumed on CPU1 after interruption on CPU0. In both partitioned and both clustered positions M resumed on CPU0.

Two additional diagnostic boots were excluded from the score. D1 (short clustered) recorded opening publication and terminal RAM showed opening Pending and reverse Empty. D2 (stream global) showed both slots Consumed. The selected M frame was IRQ-saved version 2 and the replacement P1 frame was fresh version 2.

The earlier #279 MG3 cohort qualified 3/6 short and 6/6 stream. These cohorts establish neither a timing improvement nor the cause of early termination.

Across both scored positions per selector, M's IRQ and destination ERET PCs matched at `0x82cb0` (global), `0x82cb4` (partitioned) and `0x82d40` (clustered). The reverse handoff selected CPU1 lease 4 each time, with M's IRQ-saved v2 frame on global and replacement P1's fresh v2 frame on both pinned selectors.

D1 retained 30 journal rows and D2 retained 72. D2's CPU0 IRQ and CPU1 ERET matched at `0x82cf0`, the exclusive upper loop bound. Its remote return falls outside the strict inner-loop criterion. In both diagnostics the journal and initial stack bounds reconstructed all 256 terminal RAM bytes exactly.

Rust predecessor-preservation controls passed 5/5 and Python handoff and diagnostic controls passed 6/6. The reader rejected a downgraded MG4 marker and reopened 30 historical sessions (6 MG1, 6 MG2, 12 MG3 and 6 CRC). Generic QEMU SMP emitted the expected transcript. After pyelftools was missing on the first offline SW1 read, the corrected reader matched CPU-tagged PCs from the same original boot. Physical Pi 4 qualification remains open.
