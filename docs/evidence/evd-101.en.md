# EVD-101 · Conditional wake-to-receipt latency for a finite MLFQ workload

`CHG-101` · `IMP-101` · `POL-009` · [#264](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/264) · [#265](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/265) · [한국어](evd-101.md)

Both admitted first boots demoted the packet worker through a real timer interrupt. A peer woke the sleeping worker. The ready worker crossed a boost, waited for a peer, resumed its saved frame and published the computed packet receipt. From the successful wake SVC stop through the worker's counter sample after publication, the plain guest took 2,459,842 counts and the traced guest took 2,486,422. Both met the 14,375,000-count conditional limit. Independent reconstruction covered peer queue order, owner charges and application packet outputs. [Design](../mlfq-wake-design.en-ko.md) · [Rust workload](../../kernel/src/mlfq_wake.rs)

The otherwise identical 14,374,999-count request refused before any dispatch in both modes. All four first positions met their expected outcomes. Sixteen historical guest configurations matched baseline terminal UART, terminal PC and executable sections. The latency result applies to the declared four-worker workload on one AArch64 QEMU CPU. Product workload, safety authority and board timing remain open under #1, #10 and #34.
