# EVD-110 · Ordinary timed mutex chain and FIFO integration

CHG-110 / IMP-110 / POL-014 / [#232](../ordinary-mutex-chain.md) / [#236](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/236)

On #236 integrated source 1bc463d, all eight first boots qualified (two timed-chain, two FIFO and four original PI/ceiling controls).

In plain and traced order, BASE progress at the interrupt was 1,414 and 2,052 of 16,384. Comparator-to-handler intervals were 8,431 and 8,440 ticks. Both boots resumed BASE at the interrupted PC `0x402014d4`. Each FIFO boot exercised both equal-priority arrival orders. All four original controls met their timing criterion. PI recorded 1 and 7 chunks and ceiling recorded 74 and 65. The previous combined trial qualified both chain boots (2/2) and two of four legacy controls (2/4). Physical-board blocking and timing qualification remains open.

On single-core Cortex-A72 QEMU virt/GICv2, both chain boots showed HIGH timing out at PPI30, LOW and BASE dropping from priority 4 to 2, and direct handoff of Y to LOW. BASE completed 4,194,304 reads and LOW published the same count and sum (35,651,584). Both FIFO boots produced 412 and 421 in their respective admission orders. All 140 host and 24 scoped Python tests passed. The build rejected eight invalid selectors. The full integrated source is `1bc463d88e5b69763e1268349a57e10685076020`.
