# EVD-134 · EEVDF telemetry service and wake response

CHG-134 / IMP-134 / POL-012 / [#331](../eevdf-telemetry-deployment.en-ko.md) / [#332](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/332)

`6c22596f71e956c9db8b97548dc91de8d79e43cb`

One operator boot on single-vCPU Cortex-A72 QEMU virt/GICv2 at default clock ran three linked EL1 entries, each with a 16 KiB stack. At 62.5 MHz, Q was 625,000 ticks. All five 32Q windows delivered at least 28Q (19,932,823–19,968,349 ticks). All 15 task/window absolute service errors were within 4Q. All eight PPI30 wakes reached the first work in consumer code within 4Q of its due time. The maximum was 770,390 ticks from due time and 766,597 ticks from accepted wake.

At the fifth wait, B changed from weight 1 and request Q to weight 2 and request 2Q. The 73 IRQs, 11 SVCs and all 84 ERETs matched restored PC/SP decisions. Both batch workers resumed after preemption and all three tasks drained.
