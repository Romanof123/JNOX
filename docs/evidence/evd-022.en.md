# EVD-022 · Weighted runtime, sleep and wake

[한국어](evd-022.md) · [Acceptance contract](../m4-vruntime.en.md) · [#55](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/55)

On the first cold boot, A and B received 5,655,507 and 11,334,667 counter counts across the first twelve grants. The discrepancy |2A − B| was 23,653 counts, within the published tolerance of 5,000,000. B then slept through three A grants. Waking B raised its position from 11,334,667 to 19,598,044 half-ticks and reduced its recorded lag from 8,263,377 to zero. The six recovery grants ran A,B,B,A,B,B. B’s final lag was −16,375 counter counts.

The independent verifier reproduced each of the 21 choices and charges from the samples. The external QEMU trace matched all 21 interrupted PCs, the initial dispatch and 20 subsequent frame returns. The final interrupt parked the CPU. The guest supplied timer samples, private stack addresses and monotonically advancing work counters. The observer exited 0. The outer shell exited 2 on a trailing carriage return after saving the verdict. [Full initial observation](evd-022.json)

Rust 1.95.0 produced identical release ELF bytes from the initial revision and the corrected proportional-scheduling revision. SHA-256 `d4dea231a8572c2dc732bccc422201a20383d1c5d14a2baff7cf21f201104203`. The final revision passed 93 Rust tests, 19 Python tests and 11 additional virtual boots.
