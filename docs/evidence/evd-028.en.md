[한국어](evd-028.md) · [Acceptance contract](../m1-periodic-overrun.en.md) · [#64](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/64)

A carriage return in the wrapper filename prevented the first invocation from launching. The corrected invocation produced one guest. The verifier decoded 154 rows and matched 64 exceptions and 64 returns. Under RM, B1 missed at relative tick 16 and A1 at 19. Under DM, A1 alone missed at relative tick 19. The IRQ observing the logical deadline-equality event arrived 107,151 and 118,019 counter counts late in the two epochs.

The counter ran at 62.5 MHz with 3,125,000 counts per tick. Logical deadline equality follows the calendar ordering. These measurements cover one virtual CPU. Board latency, physical deadline equality and WCET require separate evidence.
