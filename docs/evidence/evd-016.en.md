# EVD-016 · Budgeted service of submitted aperiodic requests

[한국어](evd-016.md)

The same six work units arrived at ticks 1, 3, 4 and 7. Each server had quota 2 per 4 ticks. After an empty first poll, PS waited until tick 4 while DS and SS served the first request at tick 1. Limiting SS to one pending credit merged two units at tick 3 for repayment at tick 7, postponing service that would otherwise occur at tick 5.

| Server | Served slots | Completed / pending at tick 10 (work units) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PS | 4, 5, 8, 9 | 4 / 2 |
| DS | 1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9 | 6 / 0 |
| SS | 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 | 5 / 1 |
| SS1 | 1, 3, 7, 8 | 4 / 2 |

The first independent guest produced 1,659 UART bytes and observer exit 0. In the same run 40 timer IRQs interrupted the actual worker or idle loops for each slot. Thirty-two producer SVCs established the submit, acknowledge and park paths. The verifier compared request IDs, remaining budgets, and all pending credit dates and amounts. Receipt retirement, register and stack values and progress counters were checked in guest code. Exception-return PCs came from the external trace. [UART / IRQ / SVC](evd-016.json)

An independent host calendar compared 6,144 combinations of arrivals, CPU availability and credit capacity. All 55 host tests, three malformed UART controls and four additional regression boots gave their expected outcomes. Physical interference, latency and product service guarantees require further evidence. [QEMU](evd-016-regressions.json)
