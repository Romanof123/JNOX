EDF dispatched A first. At grid 1, C preempted A, returned through its trampoline and restored A before B ran. Overrun A exhausted its estimate at grid 2 and continued with the estimate clamped at 1 until its single return. LLF dispatched B first, retained B on the 2/2 tie at grid 5 and switched to A on 1/2 at grid 6 without a release. A finished before grid 7. In the overload case C arrived at grid 7 with slack −2, interrupted B, then completed and restored B. The model refused normal admission with code 9. C finished on time in the override run. LLF B also received positive service after estimate exhaustion and completed on time. A deadline miss requires completion strictly after the absolute deadline. Deadline−1, equality and deadline+1 were checked against the linked comparison on the host.

Each IRQ was PPI 30. Every completion matched SVC #1, the trampoline return ELR, the private stack and an advancing progress witness. Every nonterminal dispatch matched the linked entry or latest saved frame and PC against QEMU ERET. The terminal decision recorded idle and parked in the dispatch completion WFE loop. The final captured PC matched its linked branch. Vector save stamp to handler took 53–2,193 counts and scheduler entry to the next restore stamp took 914–32,112. Assembly instructions outside the stamps limit exact attribution. There was at most one involuntary switch per grid. Completion redispatches were counted separately. Worker wall-time stop conditions advance during preemption. Physical minimum dwell, worst-case IRQ latency and product costs remain unmeasured. Runtime failure guards were inspected and admission refusals exercised on the host.
