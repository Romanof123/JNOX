# EVD-103 · Minimum grants in every finite lottery window

CHG-103 · IMP-103 · POL-010 · [Issue #269](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/269) · [PR #270](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/270) · [한국어](evd-103.md)

On one Cortex-A72 QEMU CPU, three continuously ready workers with ticket weights 1, 2 and 4 each processed their own packet stream. Independent replay found the following minima across **all 25 overlapping windows of 24 grants** in each 48-dispatch run. Worker A summed payload bytes, B counted bytes whose two low bits were zero, and C reported the maximum byte. Each actual dispatch produced one worker receipt. [Prospective contract](../lottery-window-plan.en-ko.md) · [Tested source 602b189](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/tree/602b1896286b9eb25b956ca1d658732441e1b719)

| Cold configuration | Minimum grants A/B/C | First outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Seed 1 | 5 / 3 / 11 | Admitted, 48 receipts |
| Seed 2 | 1 / 7 / 10 | Admitted, 48 receipts |
| Seed 1, A requires 6 | 5 / 3 / 11 | Refused before dispatch or timer IRQ. Window [24,48) gives A five grants |

Admission preserved the live draw state. Grant counts describe slot occupancy. The capture records timer compare and elapsed counter values separately. The independent campaign recorded 139 passing host tests and 8,640 independent comparisons. All six legacy configurations reproduced their expected behaviors. The lottery-miss diagnostic still observed two ticks against one at a shifted source line. The assembly benchmark ran with the correct feature after the initial selector error was recorded. All six legacy ELF hashes changed. Only the assembly benchmark matched its baseline flat image. [Bilingual first report](lottery-window-first.en-ko.md) · [Original evidence archive](lottery-window-first.zip)

The result applies to the frozen generator, seeds, ready set, packet workload and finite windows. Combined-source checks remain open under #271. Product inputs, physical-board timing and worst-case execution time require separate evidence.
