# EVD-020 · A live HI job exhausts its LO and HI budgets

[한국어](evd-020.md) · [Acceptance contract](../m3-mixed-criticality.en.md) · [#51](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/51)

The first independent cold boot ran jobs 0, 1, 1, 1, 3, 0 across six physical timer intervals. With all four slots occupied, job 1 required more than its LO budget. The runtime entered HI mode and removed active LO jobs 0 and 2. It rejected a new LO release and an early reset. Job 1 exhausted its three-unit HI budget, the fourth grant was refused, and surviving job 3 completed its sum of 528 before its deadline. After an unapproved restart was rejected, an approved reset admitted a fresh LO instance.

The verifier matched all six IRQ PCs to the linked work loops, six exception returns to job entries or saved PCs, and stack addresses to the job instances. Progress increased across both HI-job resumptions. The slot-2 compare was 6,320,305 counter ticks, IRQ sample 6,447,422, mode commit 6,458,942 and first resumed-work sample 6,493,064. Compare to resumed work took 172,759 ticks or 2.764144 ms at 62.5 MHz in this run. Survivor completion was observed at 15,827,255 ticks, before its 18,820,305-tick deadline. The external trace corroborates CPU execution. Workload, admission and reset outcomes are recorded by the guest. [Same-run UART, exceptions and independent reconstruction](evd-020.json)

The frozen commit passed 80 host Rust tests, 14 Python tests and 11 additional sequential guest boots. Physical worst-case transition latency, product carry-in and system safety approval remain open. [Combined regression record](evd-019-020-regressions.json)
