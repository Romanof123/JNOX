On the first cold boot, the two higher-class jobs alternated across four physical timer interrupts with the lower-class job ready throughout. Only after both blocked did the lower-class job run. In the feedback epoch, two early yields each consumed one unit. The worker’s remaining allotment decreased from three to two to one. A third charge demoted it behind the waiting CPU-bound peer. At service unit 12 the boost preserved ready order, and the CPU-bound job ran before the newly woken interactive job.

The fresh verifier matched all 17 outgoing PCs and selected returns to the retained ELF and exception trace. Eight physical timer IRQs and nine SVCs acted on six distinct task frames across the two epochs. Each charge equalled the whole elapsed 2,500,000-counter-unit ticks capped by the grant. The measurement interval includes dispatch return and trap entry. [Same-run UART, journal, PCs and checks](evd-019.json)

The frozen commit passed 80 host Rust tests, including an independent queue oracle covering 5,850 traces, 14 Python tests and 11 additional serialized guest boots. Two unused shared ready-queue methods produced one compiler warning in this profile. Physical timing limits and service under product workloads remain open.
