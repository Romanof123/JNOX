Nine original launches completed on single-CPU Cortex-A72 QEMU virt at 62.5 MHz. Across strict MLQ and feedback MLFQ, plain and traced, the CRC process handed off at all 12 reporter wakes. CRC progressed during sleep and the reporter verified three rounds of 256 alarms (768 total, exit 53). In the strict three-slot run the two high-class slots began 0→1→0; the low-class reporter first ran after both exited. Both main feedback runs crossed six boost thresholds.

In the cumulative control, slot 0 was demoted on yield 305 before its first boost. The equal, cumulative and solo yield captures each retained a 512-row prefix and dropped 15,939, 15,905 and 15,886 later transitions respectively. Those suffixes remain unscored. Separate terminal private RAM confirmed 16,384 blocks, 67,108,864 bytes, 4,194,304 alarms and exit 59 in each run. Three all-sleep waits contributed zero service and boosts.

All 165 host tests passed. Eleven invalid CLI and ten invalid raw Cargo cases were rejected. Scored guest rows contained no natural exact compare tie or late charge crossing multiple levels. Saved return PCs corroborate the traced handoffs; the pre-ERET counter supplies only a lower bound on first-instruction time. These observations qualify the selected virtual settings. Physical response times and product-wide bounds remain open.
