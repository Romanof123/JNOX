# EVD-045 v1 · Ordinary Rust aperiodic server timing and service

[한국어](evd-045-v1.md) · [EVD-045](evd-045.en.md)

**PARTIAL. All ten first default-clock boots failed before arbitration.** The first IRQ was 130,150–366,557 ticks late (2.0824–5.864912 ms), above the 62,500-tick limit. No submission, grant, worker completion or mask occurred in those runs. Separately, five deterministic schedules completed and two deterministic fault controls retained charged, unfinished work.

The eight default-clock ordinary selectors covered PS, DS, SS2 and SS1 for both A and B; the other two images were A/SS2-late and B/DS-late. All ten stopped on IRQ 0, guest verdict 1, without a whole missed slot. In a separate icount shift=0 series the first five normal captures timed out during UART transmission after 15 host seconds. Both first diagnostic fault captures completed. A new cold series with Linux-local UART capture retained all seven complete records. The archive keeps all 24 attempts.

FIFO receipts served A as IDs 1/2/2/3 and B as 1/1/1/1/2/2. In A/SS1 the one-entry queue merged the credit at slot 3 into two units due at slot 7. The higher-priority periodic task took slots 2/6/10/14 in every complete normal diagnostic. Its nominal-release-to-finish intervals were 258–260 ticks. IRQ lateness was zero and maximum sampled preparation through the assembly return sample was 94 ticks (1.504 µs). QEMU supplied ERET destinations but no final restore-instruction timestamps. B/PS, B/DS and B/SS2 had no normal deterministic schedule in this series.

Each unit consumes one discrete entitlement at grant. Retirement waits for the worker’s 256-step checksum and completion SVC. Both delayed controls stopped after 128 steps at an IRQ 4,687,500 ticks (75 ms) late, one whole slot beyond target. A/SS2 stopped at slot 2 with one outstanding unit; B/DS at slot 1 with four. Both charged one unit, retired zero and performed no later arbitration.

Evidence covers two finite input manifests on one virtual A72. Four resident IDs, at most six outstanding units and one to four units per request are input limits. An observed schedule supplies no independently justified execution budget for 1 ms IRQ delivery, 5 ms preparation or the 45 ms remaining service allowance. Default-clock timing, actual M3 interference, board WCET and product admission remain open.
