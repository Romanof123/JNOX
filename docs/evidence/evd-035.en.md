# EVD-035 · First-failure capture for proportional scheduling

[한국어](evd-035.md) · [#79](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/79) · [Acceptance design](../m4-proportional.en.md)

One shell invocation failed before launching QEMU. Each of the three configurations then ran once on a single virtual Cortex-A72. The ordinary and diagnostic guests each passed with 116 UART bytes, 81 applied rows, 75 IRQs and six SVCs. In both, the two lottery seeds gave A/B/C service of 10/8/14 and 5/11/16. Stride service changed from 4/8/0 to 2/4/8 after admission at floor 720,720. The delayed grant charged three ticks and advanced pass by 2,162,160. The ordinary ELF had no failure symbol. The diagnostic record was `[0,3,0,0,0]`. A zero in the first word means no committed failure. The offline verifier corrected its initial all-zero assumption using the declared format. No guest was rerun.

The forced guest emitted 45 UART bytes through PROPORTIONAL FAIL. The transcript observer exited 1 and QEMU exited 0 after capture. The sole applied row was epoch 0, slot 0. B won draw 2 in [1,3) of seven tickets. Its start, compare and stop counters were 72,495, 1,322,495 and 1,454,468. Elapsed service was 1,381,973 counts at 625,000 counts per tick. PPI30 interrupted B at PC `0x4020127c`. B had entered at `0x40201268`. Progress was 75,504,959. The five-word record `[468,0,0,2,1]` identified the source line, epoch, slot, two actual ticks and one expected tick. All subsequent rows were empty.

The historical #79 ELF recorded four IRQs in B/A/B/A at PCs `0x4020127c`, `0x40201260`, `0x4020127c`, `0x40201260`. Three handlers returned before the fourth interruption at epoch 0, slot 3. Its missing counter and predicate data leave the original cause open. These captures cover the declared QEMU fixture. Physical timing and product-level interference require separate evidence.
