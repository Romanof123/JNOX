# EVD-138 · Boot-lifetime idle refill health

CHG-138 / IMP-138 / POL-016 / [#339](../native-hierarchy-refill-health.en-ko.md) / [#340](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/340)

`a1a6a2f7440342125e257825775d4f2caacf0cd6`

Of ten candidate first positions on single-CPU Cortex-A72 virt, wake-trace recorded one genuine late idle refill. At decision 60→61 the fixed compare was 250,487,263 and earliest PPI30 entry was 260,476,531. The lag of 9,989,268 ticks (159.828288 ms) exceeded the 1,953,125-tick bound by 8,036,143. The interrupt was refill-only and idle charge was zero. This was the sole violation among 29 refills in that boot. The first-violation and maximum fields matched raw UART and RAM. Reporter, histogram and energy products matched and exited 7/70/179.

The other nine candidate first positions and two separate passive observations stayed within the refill bound. Two plain comparator boots and the default CRC control remain separate. The failing boot retained external exceptions but no passive GIC stream. The observations do not isolate assertion, WFI or host scheduling as the cause. #339 tracks these timings alongside the two original #334 misses. POL-016 remains open.

The original CLI accepted two contradictory controls. The first reader correction dfc6eb3 rejected both and preserved the natural miss on the retained firsts, but accepted nine impossible evidence overlaps.
