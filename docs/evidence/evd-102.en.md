# EVD-102 · CPU0 wake, dispatch and application receipt

CHG-102 · IMP-102 · POL-009 · CAP-013 · [한국어](evd-102.md) · [Issue #268](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/268) · [Trace design](../scheduling-app-trace-v1.en-ko.md)

The first combined campaign cold-booted 35 distinct configurations on QEMU virt. All eight trace-profile boots produced the expected UART and sealed files. The public decoder rejected all eight because the capture wrapper omitted the post-stop status observation. The original rejection remains part of the evidence. [Combined first report](sched-app-trace-joint-first.en-ko.md)

With the corrected observer, eight fresh captures across OFF, runtime-disabled, active and saturated modes, each with the legacy journal off and on, recorded the paused VM and passed public decoding. Each active stream committed 27 events. Each saturated stream retained four records and counted 22 kernel drops and one application drop. Complete intervals require the active stream. The compiled-in descriptor occupies 8,320 bytes. Dynamic stack high-water and maximum IRQ mask duration remain unmeasured. [Corrected first report](sched-app-trace-halt-corrected.en-ko.md)

The accepted wake SVC supplies stop sample W. Q is the fresh post-enqueue sample, S is the first subsequent assignment to the target, and E is the target's counter sample after it publishes its computed packet receipt. S−Q measures observed ready-to-dispatch delay. E−S includes restoration and the target's useful work. E−W is checked against the four-worker workload's conditional 23U bound alongside all four calculated packet results. Each timer pair measures its own Rust handler interval and may overlap scheduler ownership. [Scheduling bound and packet reconstruction](../mlfq-wake-design.en-ko.md)

This evidence covers partial timing, footprint, observability and scheduling behavior in a trusted linked EL1 image. REQ-011 is affected by producer and export scope. Cross-domain access enforcement, physical-board timing and product approval remain open. Review of the latest typed-input checks is pending.
