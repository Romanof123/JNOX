## Uninterrupted first boot · 2026-11-24

The uninterrupted guest contract passed in one cold boot. Four completed rows (0, 1, 2 and 4) had response times of 512.400, 66.432, 95.392 and 215.584 µs. All four had at least 599.4876 ms of deadline margin. The guest reported three load batches, and the measured load interval enclosed foreground execution. The middle batch has no separate timestamp in the witness. This verdict leaves the independent ordinary-loop PC/SP control-flow replay open.

The evidence covers this virtual boot. Physical-board timing and product functional safety require separate evidence. ELF SHA-256 `ff4864f91bd1fb9f39d6bac5208cda9742b35112149e24ca8329fabd358c879a`.
