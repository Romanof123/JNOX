# EVD-045 · Ordinary Rust aperiodic server timing and service

[한국어](evd-045.md) · [#101](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/101) · [PR #108](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/108)

**The integrated default-clock run completed all eight ordinary schedules.** Both deliberate late controls stopped after charging one unit and retiring none. The earlier standalone default-clock series stopped all ten images at their first IRQ before arbitration. The integration replay matched the per-case ELF and QEMU hashes across both series. The reason for the timing difference is unresolved. [v2](evd-045-v2.en.md) · [v1](evd-045-v1.en.md) · [Evidence index](evd-045.json)

All 34 image comparisons matched the standalone PT_LOAD layouts, loaded bytes and .text. The comparisons covered 12 multilevel images before integration with the servers, the same 12 configurations after integration, and 10 integrated aperiodic images. In the first standalone default-clock series, all eight ordinary schedules and both late controls stopped at the first IRQ with zero charge. The integrated default-clock series completed 8/8 ordinary schedules. Both injected late controls stopped after charging one unit and retiring none. Deterministic instruction counting gave the same eight completions and two intended stops in its own series. The standalone default captures used WSL DrvFS and the integrated default captures used Linux /tmp. The cause of the timing difference remains unresolved. The audit of the frozen source tree reported missing evidence links for IMP-045 and IMP-046. [Combined regression report](evd-045-regressions.json)
