# EVD-129 · Two native prime counters on the Pi 4 model

CHG-129 / IMP-129 / POL-002 / [#321](../pi4-native-fixed-applications.en-ko.md) / [#322](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/322)

The original candidate `001dda0` failed to assemble because it defined the local label `.Lpark` twice. No guest booted from that build. The corrected candidate `d905d924a90ea1041821bac78919194852263fe5` packaged the C and Rust ELFs at priority 2. The first four corrected model boots reported exit statuses 12 and 77.

FIFO recorded zero equal-priority timer rotations in both plain and traced boots. RR recorded 16 in each, including A→B→A before either process exited. The quantum was 1,250,000 ticks at 62.5 MHz. In the traced run, IRQ/ERET observations matched the PCs and stacks of the interrupted and restored workers. Only CPU0 took recorded EL0 exceptions. CPUs 1–3 ended at park PC 0x310.

In all four boots, independent walks of the two process page tables resolved VA 0x402000 to PA 0x2036000 and 0x244b000. Terminal kernel RAM supplied the C and Rust prime counts of 78,498 and 70,435 and the exit slots 12 and 77. Each process had a separate stack.

Both staged firmware trees matched the pinned hashes and raw image bytes. A pair of 8 MiB ELF containers also booted and exited 12/77. A C source primary and sensor peer received separate argument lists, yielded and exited 17/29. The frozen ELFs completed on virt FIFO and RR, and the existing linked Pi FIFO smoke completed. The report preserves the initial host archive CRLF and missing Git metadata failures and the linked-smoke wrapper failure before guest launch.

QEMU 11.1.3 raspi4b direct loading bypasses EEPROM, SD and VideoCore firmware execution. Physical power cycles, UART, board timing and product acceptance remain open.
