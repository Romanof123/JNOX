## Scheduling policy inputs

At tick 100, nine jobs yield nine distinct winners. FCFS, fixed priority, SJF, SRTF, HRRN, RM, DM, EDF and LLF choose IDs 0 through 8 respectively. Each expected winner begins at the back of its queue. On the same guest seven further suites check budgets and stride, cyclic windows and hard CBS, weighted virtual time and EEVDF, feedback and lottery, aperiodic servers, CPU placement and criticality, and group budgets and policy fallback. The source fixtures retain exact inputs and expected results. [Ranking workload](../../kernel/src/dispatch_policy.rs) · [Policy references and release criteria](../scheduling-policies.en.md)
