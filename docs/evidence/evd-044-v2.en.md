The P and Q ELFs were byte-identical to their respective v1 images. The stricter checker matched ELF initial PC/SP, private stacks, independent IRQ/return PCs and worker recurrence. Peak delays were 0.768560 ms for P and 0.704816 ms for Q. Both original v1 streams still returned status 2. The observed difference has no established cause.

Separate mutations of the accumulator, first PC, generation, next boundary and saved PC each returned status 1. All six historical synthetic streams were rejected under the stricter accumulator and initial-frame checks. The current generator produced six valid P/Q controls with the expected healthy, just-over-limit and skipped-slot outcomes. Eight host tests were recorded. The wire format carries no run ID. The checker correlates table, generation, ELF digest and independent IRQ/return order. The separately archived assembly fixture belongs to [#112](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/112).

After following the JSON restoration steps, replay the two recorded guests in a POSIX shell with AArch64 nm installed.

```sh
for t in P Q; do
  lower=$(printf '%s' "$t" | tr 'PQ' 'pq')
  run="partition-restored/partition-select-fresh/104-ordinary-partition-$lower"
  digest=$(python3 -c 'import json,sys;print(json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))["elf_sha256"])' "$run/execution.json")
  python3 partition-restored/frozen-source/tools/ordinary_partition_account.py "$run/uart" --origin guest --case healthy --table "$t" --elf "$run/kernel.elf" --elf-sha256 "$digest" --exceptions "$run/exceptions.log" || exit 1
done
```
