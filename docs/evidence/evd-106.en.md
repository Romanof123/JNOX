# EVD-106 · CPU-local dispatch leases and activation TLS

CHG-106 / IMP-106 / POL-013 / [#278](../boards/pi4-migration-local-state.en-ko.md) / [#279](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/279) / [#47](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/47) / [#21](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/21)

On frozen #279 source ce8aebc, the public MG3 reader qualified 3/6 short first boots and 6/6 stream first boots. Short global 2 resumed on CPU1. Partitioned 1 and clustered 2 resumed on CPU0.

The #278 plan froze separate CPU dispatch leases, four activation TLS objects and MG3 saved/live checks before the scored runs. Independent review confirmed saved and live TLS, matching CPU-tagged IRQ and ERET return PCs, and recomputed checkpoint prefixes in all 12 positions. Nine met the strict workload criteria. The three remaining short positions (global 1, partitioned 2, clustered 1) all restored execution outside the strict loop criteria. None finished before the interrupt. Historical archive replay separately scored MG1 short 1/6, MG2 short 0/6 and CRC stream 6/6. The generic virt control reported SMP PASS. Pi SW1 reached its terminal record.

For the three short misses, the destination recorded a live M checkpoint and the return matched the saved TLS and PC. Global 1 returned at `0x801e4` outside `[0x82304,0x82328)`, partitioned 2 at `0x82368` outside `[0x82308,0x8232c)`, and clustered 1 at `0x82088` outside `[0x823b4,0x823d8)`. In the generic virt control the task cookie survived CPU0→CPU1 migration. The bounded public Pi SW1 replay exited 0 and matched the CPU-tagged PCs using the original 30-second ceiling and one-second quiet interval. The initial fixed-duration SW1 smoke capture is separate.
