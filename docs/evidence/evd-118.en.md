# EVD-118 · Recurring native jobs under RM and DM

CHG-118 / IMP-118 / POL-004 / [#300](../native-periodic-applications.en-ko.md) / [#301](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/301)

The same C and Rust ELF pair started C first under RM and Rust first under DM; both applications exited with their expected statuses of 17 and 29. The first plain QEMU boots ran at 62.5 MHz on source `4a0c17a7025d663d90b67535ccc27b594277ec08`. Historical #94 outcomes, product workload assumptions and physical-board qualification remain independently tracked.

Among the 18 original positions, the DM overrun selected B1 at a due clock syscall (cause 5), retained A’s frame and resumed A at PC `0x4001a0`, SP `0x1010f40`. In the original overrun, RM missed A1 and B1; DM missed A1 alone. The sleep-backlog case cancelled three generations. Its plain boot missed g0 through g2 and its traced boot missed g0 through g3.

Four supplemental first boots used a fixed 32 MiB A1 workload with independently checked CRC `0xfa914cb6`. Under DM, B1 and B2 interrupted busy A1 via a real EL0 timer IRQ at `0x4001ec` in crc_ramp, SP `0x1010f50`. The external IRQ/ERET trace confirmed A resumed with the same frame. B1 finished 2,466,751 ticks before its deadline in plain DM and 2,231,856 ticks before it in traced DM. Under RM, B1 finished 7,744,212 and 8,914,655 ticks late, respectively. Fixed-workload misses were A1/B1/B2 for plain RM, A1/A2/B1/B2 for traced RM, A1 for plain DM and A1/A2 for traced DM. The fixed traced case cancelled A3.

All 152 host policy cases passed and 11 invalid packages were rejected. Native startup and EDF controls exited 37 and 43 on both parent and candidate. The historical linked RM/DM default loss observer reported its original actual-delivery failure on both sources.

## Original candidate disposition

Release review held source `4a0c17a7025d663d90b67535ccc27b594277ec08` because processing an observation before a completion at the same counter could record a spurious overlap or cancellation. Of the original 11 rejected packages, several reached only the generic selector error. Separate targeted controls used real ELFs in 17 CLI checks and exercised 10 direct Cargo diagnostics. Two older linked RM control ELFs were overwritten. Their original hashes, UART captures, logs and statuses were retained.

## Corrected calendar ordering

Source `770c19b` follows the `fbbae9e` equal-counter rule. An independent explicit-generation oracle checked 1,664 snapshots of releases, completions, overlaps, misses, cancellations and the running owner. It covered 96 same-rank chronology cases across RM and DM, six slot permutations, four duplicate counts, wait and exit, plus an actual owner change and a genuinely later completion. The same oracle failed on the original source and passed on the correction. There were 161 serial host cases including the two generated suites. In 22 fresh guest boots the verifier checked the reference RM/DM order, ranking inversions, fixed-data IRQ interruption and in-loop restoration, and worker products. The startup and EDF controls exited 37 and 43 on both base and candidate. The linked loss-control observations remain separate.
