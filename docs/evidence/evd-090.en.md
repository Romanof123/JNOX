# `EVD-090` · Static ELF footprint measurement

`VER-009` covers the static part of `REQ-009` here. At `34454f955a30b0d3be0a4dfc73435a344f2dbff9` the minimal virt image measured 275 B of RX and 20,496 B of static RAM, within the inclusive 256 KiB and 2 MiB limits. Fixed-RR measured 14,728 B and 115,056 B as a comparator without its own ceiling. The first checker accepted a misaligned BSS end and rejected a valid retained read-only section.
