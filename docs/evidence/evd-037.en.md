# EVD-037 · Ordinary batch jobs under SJF, SRTF and HRRN

[한국어](evd-037.md) · [Application contract](../ordinary-batch.en.md) · [#81](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/81) · [#86](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/86)

Each policy passed two ordinary boots and a separate instrumented QMP boot. All three first independent replays passed. The instrumented runs yielded 60 decisions: per policy one initial dispatch, two IRQs and 17 SVCs. The verifier matched UART events against the RAM witness, reconstructed readiness and service, and compared selected owners, timer compares, saved frame addresses and return PCs.

At the first IRQ, SJF and HRRN retained owner 1 on frame `0x4020de50`. SRTF switched from that frame to owner 4 on `0x40219ec0`, then restored owner 1 after the shorter job completed. Once owner 1 completed, SJF selected owners 4/2/3 in order and HRRN selected 2/3/4. All three later dispatched owner 5 from idle on the second IRQ.

Charged counter ticks are elapsed intervals between decisions, including kernel overhead. Owner 0 denotes idle.

Each policy rejected estimates 0 and 2³², an overflowing delay, and a fifth live job with status codes 1/1/4/2. Completion attempts using another live generation and a retired generation left the caller running. Generation 5 reused generation 1’s stack, exceeded its estimate, and retained remaining rank one when it submitted generation 6. All six admitted generations completed. The evidence covers this four-slot trusted application on one virtual CPU. It establishes neither WCET nor a production capacity bound.
