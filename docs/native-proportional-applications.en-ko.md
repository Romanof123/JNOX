## Operator interface / 실행 옵션

~~~sh
python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out lottery1 --policy lottery --quantum-ticks 625000 --lottery-seed 1 --lottery-window 48,24 --lottery-floors 5,3,11 --elf crc.elf --weight 1 --peer-elf histogram.elf --peer-weight 2 --peer2-elf energy.elf --peer2-weight 4
python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out stride --policy stride --quantum-ticks 625000 --elf crc.elf --weight 1 --peer-elf histogram.elf --peer-weight 2 --peer2-elf energy.elf --peer2-weight 4
~~~

Use normal slot order A/B/C, one explicit integer weight 1..16 per present ELF, quantum 1..i64::MAX, explicit decimal u64 seed including zero for lottery; reject seed or lottery bounds on stride. Optional window H,W and ordered per-slot floors must be supplied together. Accept 1≤W≤H≤64 and nonnegative integer floors summing to at most W; reject inconsistent counts, duplicated settings, overflow, unsupported board/profile and malformed numbers at public and raw interfaces. Preserve default policies, primary C input and existing --arg/peer argument handling. Record exact ELF identities, seed/algorithm, class of guarantee, weights, masks, window/floors, quantum and unit in the manifest and guest. Invalid numeric configuration or impossible requested floors must fail before the first EL0 restore or operational timer arm. Do not publish a ready-to-run package for a public admission failure.

The optional floor is a conditional **grant-count** contract for the first H consecutive selections with every configured owner continually ready and each returning a full timer grant. Before launch independently enumerable simulation uses a copy of the exact seeded initial state. Check every W-wide interval; report its first deficient index, owner, count and floor. Leave the actual runtime RNG state untouched. A job blocking, yielding, exiting or otherwise truncating a lease before that horizon invalidates the all-ready window claim; continue normal service and expose the first such event. No global bound on waiting time, unpredictable entropy, statistical SLA, WCET or CPU-time floor follows from a finite draw sequence.

운영자는 ELF마다 가중치 1~16, 공통 퀀텀과 추첨 시드 하나를 정한다. 선택적인 창 하한은 고정 시드·계속 준비된 실행자·온전한 타이머 배정이라는 조건에서만 성립한다. 부팅 전 모의 계산은 실제 난수 상태를 바꾸지 않으며 조건이 깨져도 정상 앱 실행은 계속한다.

## Decisions and accounting / 선택과 정산

Use the released SplitMix64 transition, fixed gamma/mix constants and wrapping state exactly as in Proportional. For positive ready-weight sum T reject raw words below 2⁶⁴ mod T, take raw mod T, and map ticket to half-open cumulative eligible intervals in slot order. Do not advance on idle. Exactly one draw per actual nonidle selection, including a single eligible slot or a repeated winner. Record state before/after, words consumed, accepted raw word, total, ticket and winner; reproduce every rejected raw word from the prior state. Freeze first/last interval and rejected-word arithmetic controls. No sampled/reseeded host randomness.

At a dispatch R arm the earlier of checked R+Q and next sleeper deadline. Require physical timer IRQ 30 at or after programmed compare, source event and actual frame. A successful future sleep, explicit yield or exit closes the active lease at its entry sample; wake all due sleepers exactly once before selection. A timer wake can close a partial grant, including one which reselects the same owner. A nonblocking ordinary syscall returns to its current lease without a fresh ticket or pass reset; preserve its anchored compare and disclose that any kernel time inside the original bracket contributes to the observed interval. If a deadline matures during a syscall, resolve it before resuming and report the actual handling event, charge once, never count it as a fictitious PPI. No draws/charges while all blocked; idle arms only the first due wake. For a solo ready app use the normal quantum and real progress, allow normal exit. No catch-up grants or inserted delay on late delivery. Report zero positive service if an observation has identical endpoints, without inventing a tick.

For stride, select the eligible minimum pass (slot ID breaks ties). Normalize by the chosen minimum before dispatch, subtracting the same offset from ready passes and retaining positive sleeper debt with the shared floor rule. On a ready-mask change use the pre-change ready minimum and place a joining/waking slot at max(saved pass, previous ready floor); when transitioning to all-idle preserve relative debt as the existing shared policy does. Apply once per actual readiness transition in a documented stable order. Charge actual observed counter ticks dt at closure with exact increment dt×(720720/weight), all weights divide the scale. Do not round to whole quanta, erase subquantum residuals on yield or cap long leases at Q. Widen the shared stop arithmetic or use an exactly equivalent checked representation: dt may exceed u32::MAX, calculate the product and addition in at least u128, normalize without distorting active differences, and fail explicitly before mutation if unrepresentable. Do not split one observed huge lease into made-up dispatches. Expose post-charge, pre/post-normalization passes, common offset and cumulative raw service for each owner. Existing linked callers retain their unit semantics.

기존 SplitMix64 및 편향 없는 구간 축소를 그대로 사용하고 실제 선택마다 한 번만 추첨한다. 스트라이드는 실제 양의 실행 간격 전체를 720720/가중치로 환산한다. 양보·대기·늦은 타이머에서도 잔여량을 버리지 않는다. u32를 넘는 구간의 계산과 실패 시 상태 보존을 별도로 확인한다.

## Adopted products and bounds / 실제 작업과 허용치

Use the exact extended [CRC, density histogram and energy inputs](weighted-analytics-deployment.en-ko.md) without a clock read or scheduling syscall during each reduction. CRC processes 134,217,728 bytes, returns CRC-32 2,124,873,618 and exits 237. Histogram processes 536,870,912 bytes, publishes nine bins 2,097,152×binomial(8,k) and exits 70. Energy processes 1,073,741,824 signed samples, publishes 5,864,240,971,776 and exits 179. Verify block/byte counts and each output with an independent oracle, compare private physical backing and actual resumed PC/SP to the linked disassembly. Reuse identical ELFs and dataset hashes for the two lottery seeds and stride. Freeze source, compiler, quantum 625,000, package/argv, normal default clock and first positions before launch.

1. **Lottery.** First cold seed 1 and seed 2 on A/B/C weights 1/2/4, H=48, W=24. All 48 selections must yield actual full timer closures (including consecutive same-owner turns), with the complete ready mask throughout and no kernel-synthesized winners. Regenerate all 48 winners and all 25 half-open windows. Seed 1 minima are 5/3/11 and totals 11/14/23. Seed 2 minima are 1/7/10 and totals 8/17/23. Separate seed-1 request 6/3/11 fails at [24,48) for A=5. Document how to invoke this refusal through the normal packaging interface and test that bypassing the CLI cannot start a refused raw configuration. Report observed counter service by owner separately from counts, including tail to actual exits.
2. **Stride stable mix.** From equal initial passes score the first 48 complete real timer grants with all three continuously ready; record every prefix and every dt, including overshoot. Let S_i(n) be actual charged ticks through prefix n, and M_n=max over measured leases j≤n of dt_j/w_owner(j). At every prefix require max_i(S_i/w_i)−min_i(S_i/w_i)≤M_n by exact integer/rational comparison: each selection starts at the smallest normalized service, and its step can increase the prior range to at most its own increment. Common pass subtraction preserves this difference. Consequently each owner's absolute deviation from w_i×sum(S)/sum(w) is ≤w_i×M_n. Report exact observed M and maximum spread/error with totals and raw service; this algorithmic fairness bound depends on actual maximum lease and establishes no hard timing limit on delivery. Reconstruct actual pass transitions after the horizon to process exits.
3. **Dynamic selection.** Use an ordinary finite SDK reporter with CRC as continuous peer at frozen weights 1:2. Reporter performs three rounds: reduce a 4,096-byte ramp x[i]=(17i+3+r) mod 256, count values ≥240, publish one complete private round, voluntarily yield, then sleep until its actual monotonic read +40 ms. Record post-wake user timestamps, three waits/returns and final count 768 (256 each), exit 53. Score this pair once under lottery seed 1 and once under stride. Require productive background work while the reporter sleeps, no charge or selection for a blocked owner and true late re-entry on real PPI. In stride observe actual distinct lease lengths, at least one positive charge shorter than Q, the correct wake pass and subsequent dispatch according to the two ready passes. Reconstruct actual masks/draws under lottery; no fixed all-ready minima apply here. Verify final voluntary exits and private results. One separate solo stock client confirms one-slot behavior. Host arithmetic controls: equal-pass ties, empty/rejoin, cutoff and last ticket, normalization with sleeping debt, dt=u32::MAX and +1, oversized multiply/add, no partially changed state.

원래 48회·24폭 창 25개를 동일 조건으로 네이티브 프로세스에서 확인한다. 세 앱의 실물 데이터·출력·복귀 프레임을 검사한다. 스트라이드는 매 접두 구간에서 가중치로 나눈 서비스량 차이를 실제 최대 단일 증가량 이하로 확인한다. 별도 수면·양보 작업의 동적 준비 상태와 추첨 결과를 고정 창 보장과 구별한다.
