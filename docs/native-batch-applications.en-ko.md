# Native batch applications / 독립 네이티브 배치 작업

[#341](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/341) · POL-003. Baseline 2cdbe3bf2e8604be54c6851046d6d95d2e106f7c. One AArch64 QEMU virt Cortex-A72 CPU, GICv2, TCG default clock, 128 MiB. Each preloaded ordinary ELF is one finite batch job. Enable independent arrivals from the physical timer without IPC, runtime job submission or privileged application code. [Shared ranking](../kernel/src/batch_policy.rs) · [Original functional cases](ordinary-batch.en.md) · [Admitted linked job mix](batch-telemetry-profile.en-ko.md)

## Operator / 운영자

~~~sh
python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out arrival --policy srtf --elf batch.elf --arg=--blocks --arg 32768 --batch-job 0,18750000 --peer-elf urgent.elf --peer-batch-job 6250000,625000
~~~

Select sjf, srtf or hrrn. Supply one --batch-job offset,estimate for main and one repeated --peer-batch-job offset,estimate per repeated --peer-elf in order, up to three slots total, including solo. Keep normal C-source/ELF and per-slot argv paths. Both decimal fields are physical counter ticks: initial ready offset 0..i64::MAX from common E and starting service estimate 1..i64::MAX. Validate type/field counts, duplicate main setting, overflow, unsupported combinations and extraneous slots at CLI and raw build; check E+offset against actual counter representation before any user entry or operational arm. Duplicated tuples are legal for different slots. Record policy, ordered ELF/argv/tuples, F, E and image identities. Estimates control ranking only. Neither completion nor forced eviction occurs when a job exhausts its estimate.

운영자는 작업마다 최초 준비 오프셋과 양의 서비스 추정치를 카운터 틱으로 지정한다. 네 번째 입력의 패키지 거부와 연결형 스케줄러의 실행 중 용량 거부는 서로 다른 기록이다. 이 경로로 외부의 비예정 요청을 제출하지 않는다.

## Execution and accounting / 실행·정산

Load through the ordinary native MMU/frame path; initialize unreleased, ready, running, sleeping and exited states. Set one future epoch E after preparation; enter kernel idle and start via first actual PPI30. Program earliest future release/wake, disable unused compare, and charge nothing to a process in idle. Admit all events due at the handling counter; sort by declared due time, then first-release before wake at equal time, then slot. Record both due and actual handling; one real decision even if several arrivals were coalesced, no fictional intervening execution. At every actual scheduling boundary sample the outgoing counter and charge only that owner. Distinguish physical occupancy between last return sample and next stopping entry, rank accounting, scheduler gaps and idle. Use checked arithmetic before state changes; preserve full dt, and clamp remaining estimate to max(1, estimate−charged service) while live. Never charge a sleep/wait interval or a different owner's work. When a syscall crosses a pending release, reconcile due events at decision time before return; retain entry/decision distinction and avoid duplicate delivery on a stale IRQ.

Use BatchScheduler and the shared dispatch comparison. Extend accounting/ready-state transitions narrowly for native suspend/resume and real service intervals; preserve linked release_due and existing linked behavior. SJF selects the smallest original estimate at a nonpreemptive dispatch. SRTF compares remaining estimates and preempts only for a strictly smaller value; tie favors the incumbent and an involuntarily preempted owner keeps its place ahead of waiting equals. HRRN at nonpreemptive dispatch maximizes 1+eligible_wait/original_estimate by checked exact cross-products. An initially waiting job ages from its declared due time, including delayed timer delivery. For a job voluntarily suspended or requeued, preserve work already received and cumulative time spent runnable and waiting; awake ready time after the declared wake counts, time asleep, executing or exited does not. Do not infer post-underestimate waiting from a clamped estimate. Tied queued ranks preserve admission order.

Explicit yield surrenders incumbent preference, requeues caller behind waiting equals and performs normal policy selection; an outranking yielding job may be selected again. A valid future absolute sleep removes the caller until its wake; waking appends in ready-event order. An elapsed sleep or ordinary clock read preserves the current owner absent a genuinely due scheduling event; do not treat repeated reads as yield or reset its estimate. Exit closes the real lease, publishes the normal status and retires that slot permanently. Stop when all jobs exit. No quantum polling or invented periodic preemptions. Arm real timer only for due releases/wakes. Retain saved/selected PC/SP, integer frame, address-space identity and actual ERET; a change in rank alone cannot establish useful resumed work.

SJF·HRRN은 다른 작업 도착만으로 실행자를 바꾸지 않는다. SRTF는 실제 남은 추정치가 엄격히 작을 때만 선점한다. 자발적 양보는 동률 순번을 내주고, 수면은 실제 준비 집합에서 제외한다. 지연 도착은 약속한 최초 시각부터 노화시키되 수면·다른 작업 서비스는 실행 시간으로 정산하지 않는다.

## Frozen normal cases / 사전 고정 실행

All counts below use F=62,500,000 Hz. Freeze the toolchain, hashes and disassembly before the first run. Use [existing SDK applications](../examples/native-edf/README.md) and the [fixed-input reduction](native-edf-block-input.en-ko.md). Capture all first attempts, including a terminal pass that missed an intended causal observation.

1. **Arrival and restoration.** Use the example command above for all three policies (change only policy/output). A sums 32,768 ramps of 4,096 samples: 128 MiB, result 17,112,760,320, exit 17. At E+6,250,000 release B, which processes eight alarm blocks, count 2,048, exit 43. Require A inside its productive reduction when PPI30 actually delivers B and its remaining rank greater than B's 625,000. SJF/HRRN retain A until its real exit; SRTF returns to B, then restores A's saved PC/SP and private mapping. Verify A's progress before interruption and after restoration and both completed products independently. Collect plain and external exception-traced first boots per policy.
2. **HRRN aging.** Use A=batch.elf in its stock no-argument 160 ms mode with tuple (0,1), B=urgent.elf (0,3125000), C=stock examples/native-llf/checksum.c (9375000,625000). A and B start tied at response ratio 1; initial admission order chooses A. C arrives at E+150 ms. Require all three live with A retained until exit, then B first under HRRN although C is shorter. At A exit t, verify t≥E+9375000 and (t−E)×625000 > (t−E−9375000)×3125000 from the actual counters (equivalently t−E<11718750). If the observed values do not distinguish the cases, retain the unqualified result. Run the same tuple under SJF as contrast: after A exits C precedes B. A publishes n complete ramps and sum 522240n, exit 17; B returns 2,048/43, C returns 16 completed blocks, sum 8,355,840, exit 17. Check each process by slot/ELF, not just status.
3. **Underestimate and ties.** In SRTF repeat case 1 with both estimates one tick. At B's release require actual A service >1 and A still running at residual 1; B ties and cannot displace it. Both products and exits required. For each policy corroborate equal queued ordering on native jobs: use A=stock timed batch (0,1), B=urgent and C=short checksum both (3125000,625000). Both B/C arrive together, A retains; B then C win the equal waiting ranks. All three supply complete outputs. Keep the earlier linked exact 4-vs-2 and 5-waiting-10-vs-fresh-1 arithmetic controls independently attributable.
4. **Lifecycle.** SRTF with examples/native-edf/sleep_first.c and sleep_last.c, both (0,1), starts A. Its yield gives B an actual turn. B's long absolute sleep lets A resume, A's 40 ms absolute wait leaves real idle; B uses 140 ms, an elapsed sleep and a final yield. Verify real wake counters, preserved residuals, zero service while blocked, timer return from idle, unchanged private values and final results 53/59. One solo positive with offset >0 must enter only after the armed release. Add bounded host controls for due-time aging after late IRQ, equal events, actual charge beyond estimate, zero/overflow, duplicate events and absence of a fourth native slot. Do not inject a timer delay in the ordinary positions.

## Evidence and decisions / 근거와 승인

Provide actual public build, boot, capture and offline replay commands. Retain normal UART/status, private terminal RAM, packet/array outputs, requested and actual event counter, ready/released mask and queue order, remaining rank and accrued eligible wait per slot, charged process service, CVAL/IAR, selected/returned frame and external vector/ERET order where traced. Account for any bounded-journal drops explicitly; a missing causal interval is incomplete. Run ordinary nonbatch compatibility and keep [#211](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/211)/[#214](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/pull/214)'s actual linked coordinator plus three-job runtime saturation refusal, application validation and exact telemetry outputs under their original selectors. Native static admission alone does not reproduce that runtime interaction. Product owner [#1](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/1) selects actual deployed mix and capacity. PM/Release may decide a named finite QEMU deployment; physical service costs, WCET and customer deployment need their own evidence under [#21](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/21).

각 최초 결과에 작업별 실제 계산값과 선점·복귀 문맥을 연결한다. 추정치 소진 뒤에도 결과를 끝까지 확인한다. 기존 연결형의 동적 수용 거부는 그 실행에서만 입증되며, 실배포 구성·용량과 WCET는 별도 승인 대상으로 남긴다.
