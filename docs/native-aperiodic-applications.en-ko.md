# Native aperiodic CPU servers / 네이티브 비주기 CPU 서버

[#325](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/325) · CHG-131 · POL-007 · baseline de16634b92a534806166affa3f1407981e1e8c75. An operator assigns one independently linked EL0 application a bounded number of processor slots while a second, independently linked foreground-independent workload uses the balance. Target for this engineering workload is one Cortex-A72 QEMU virt CPU, GICv2, 128 MiB, TCG default clock. Both programs use the existing SDK, private address spaces and timer. [Existing server implementation](../kernel/src/aperiodic_policy.rs) · [Linked workload and cost gates](pol-007-rust-server-plan.md) · [Admission provenance](https://github.com/Romanof123/JNOX/issues/203)

## Package / 패키지

~~~sh
python3 tools/build_native_el0.py --out ss --policy sporadic --server-slot-ms 50 --server-budget 2,5 --server-credits 2 --elf service.elf --peer-elf background.elf
~~~

Select polling, deferrable or sporadic. The primary ELF is the service, the peer the background application. Require exactly two ordinary single-threaded ELFs on virt; accept ordinary application arguments and ELF validation. The slot length is a positive decimal millisecond value. The budget tuple is positive quota Q and period P in slots, 1 ≤ Q ≤ P, both u32. Sporadic accepts an explicit bounded credit capacity (1..8); reject that option for the other policies. Reject duplicate or malformed options, incompatible policy/board/third process, unsupported frequency conversion or representational overflow. Check at the CLI and direct build/runtime layers as applicable. Freeze the immutable settings and ELF hashes in the manifest and echo the selected settings on the guest. Arbitrary user programs run without example symbols or IPC. Provide concise invocations for both other policies, reporting units and how to read exhaustion and replenishment.

운영자가 독립 서비스 ELF와 일반 작업 ELF를 지정한다. 슬롯 길이는 밀리초, 예산과 보충 주기는 슬롯 수다. 산발 서버의 보충 기록 개수는 1~8로 제한한다. 예제 출력이나 IPC에 의존하지 않고 기존 앱 실행·격리 경로를 사용한다.

## CPU semantics / 프로세서 배정

Freeze future epoch E after startup, slot H = slot_ms × F/1000 with checked exact conversion, arm absolute E+kH. Both processes start ready. On each actual grid resolve due wakes, mature credits/refill and decide once. At most one service grant can be made in the cell. The running service consumes one whole logical unit on grant; a sleep, yield or exit cannot return that unit. Remaining raw counter residency, exception handling and idle are measured independently. A grant confers execution within this slot only. Yield or blocking surrenders its remainder to ready background or kernel idle; waking later in the cell makes the service eligible at the next grid. A normal non-scheduling syscall leaves its owner eligible to continue inside the active grant. If the service is unavailable or unfunded at the grid, run ready background, otherwise idle. Background sleep/wake/exit does not generate server budget. A ready background can resume in an unused tail or after a real wake. Stop when both applications exit. Use existing monotonic absolute sleep, real PPI30, per-process frame and translation-root switching. Preserve preempted state, exact saved PC/SP and arm the earliest grid or actual wake. A syscall arriving at a boundary must reconcile the current slot before any return; do not duplicate that debit on pending IRQ.

Use AperiodicServer's actual refill, charge and bounded-credit transitions. Its existing release(work) models finitely queued work; an executing live process supplies persistent runnable demand. Factor or adapt the shared tick transaction to represent this explicitly. While a runnable service is selected it still has demand for the next decision until it actually blocks/exits. Fabricating one queued token per tick would drain polling credits on every grant. For polling an empty poll forfeits the available quota, and observed drain/surrender forfeits its remaining current-period eligibility; a later wake cannot recreate that balance. Deferrable preserves unused quota until the next period opening (reset to Q, no accumulation). Sporadic spends its initial Q and schedules one credit due at charged k+P. Mature due credits before deciding. When the credit queue is full, merge into the youngest entry, adding one unit and moving the combined due date to k+P. Older entries retain their dates. Publish each pending (due,amount) and enforce conservation for sporadic. Preserve existing finite-backlog callers' behavior and arithmetic failure semantics.

For a late real timer observation show intended and delivered cell, real interrupted owner, elapsed occupancy, skipped indices and spill. Advance skipped ledger slots as unavailable, maturing/releasing credit from actual prior debits without inventing work or assigning fictitious grants; compute bounded catch-up or fail visibly on an unrepresentable gap. Retain the original epoch. Report missed cells, lost records or entry/return across an unhandled boundary as unqualified for healthy timing. Use bounded storage, including explicit overflow; do not allow log saturation to silently manufacture a complete timeline. Production scheduling does not depend on output being collected.

실제 준비·차단·종료 상태가 수요다. 서비스에 배정한 슬롯은 조기 대기나 종료 뒤에도 한 단위로 차감한다. 남은 CPU 시간에는 일반 작업 또는 커널 유휴 문맥이 실행된다. 폴링은 빈 수요에서 잔여 예산을 잃고, 연기 가능 서버는 주기 경계까지 보존한다. 산발 서버는 실제 차감한 시각에 따라 보충하고 큐가 차면 가장 늦은 보충을 새 만기로 미룬다. 놓친 슬롯은 가상 실행으로 채우지 않는다.

## Frozen application cases / 고정 응용 부하

Use the same two ordinary ELF hashes, inputs and arguments for all four server configurations. Use F divisible by 1000, H=50 ms, Q=2, P=5. In its first activation the service samples its actual monotonic time T, calculates a useful private checksum over bytes 0..255, records it, then requests an absolute sleep until T+125 ms. Require this sleep in slot 0 and the target strictly within cell 2, followed by first eligible grid k=3. After actual sleep return, repeatedly sum squares of 0..255 in private completed blocks; after each full block publish its count and sum and check time. Finish at the first complete block at/after T+650 ms, exit 41. Expect initial checksum 32,640 and final square sum 5,559,680 × completed blocks, with positive work in the post-wake grants. Background independently computes popcount-density histograms of complete 0..255 permutations, terminating after a full block at/after 900 ms from its own first entry, exit 42. Verify positive blocks, all nine bins = (1,8,28,56,70,56,28,8,1) × blocks, progress across service suspensions and no updates by the other PID. Sample the clock at practical batch intervals if a batch is the published count unit; preserve exact block-level product and freeze loop bounds before first execution. Only ordinary clock/sleep/exit calls are needed.

| Mode | Service grant indices within [0,10) | Distinguishing events |
|---|---|---|
| Polling | 0,5,6 | The initial partial use drains readiness; a real wake in cell 2 gets no grant at 3 or 4. |
| Deferrable | 0,3,5,6 | The remaining unit survives the sleep and runs at 3; period opening at 5 resets to two. |
| Sporadic, two credits | 0,3,5,8 | Initial charges mature separately at 5 and 8. |
| Sporadic, one credit | 0,3,8,9 | Charge 3 moves the old credit due at 5 into a two-unit credit due at 8. Cell 5 has no funds. |

Cells with no grant run ready background; after the initial sleep the background also receives the unspent cell-0 tail. Require independent application progress under all four modes, actual user returns and both final products/statuses. Exact grant sequences above depend on the named sleep landing in cell 2 and continued demand through 9. Capture actual timing and retain a miss if the prerequisites do not occur. No synthetic wake, mock worker, injected interrupt mask or inferred completion can meet these named cases.

같은 두 ELF와 같은 인자에서 최초 수면은 슬롯 2 안에 만료된다. 실제 슬롯 3 이후의 서비스, 일반 작업 진척, 잔여 예산 및 각 보충 시각을 대조한다. 정상 결과는 각 앱의 완료 블록 수로 재계산한 개인 산출물과 종료값 41·42다. 표의 순서를 실제로 관찰하지 못한 첫 실행도 보관한다.

## Return eligibility correction / 실제 복귀 직전 자격 확인

In the original sporadic two-credit plain boot, background clock syscall event 124 selected before counter 31689072, programmed the compare by 31689292 and returned at 31689350. The next physical IRQ arrived at 31692361. Preserve this original interval. The corrected EL0 restore and EL1 idle transfer sample CNTPCT immediately before committing to the destination. At or beyond the earliest pending grid/wake, retain the whole prepared frame, resample readiness and retry. A retry is kernel preparation. It performs no user operation, GIC acknowledge or EOI. An unentered new service grant that crosses its cell end is undone, including the associated sporadic credit. A wake in the same cell can retain a provisional grant and cannot spend a second unit. After the passing sample, the final store, scratch-register and stack restoration, and ERET or idle branch still take time. The stamp bounds the transfer from below; the external exception record and actual destination execution remain independently necessary.

CONFIG v2 retains the original EVENT columns 0..36. Column 37 identifies an unentered preparation (0), a delivered transfer (1) or terminal park (2). Column 38 holds the rejecting final counter sample, zero when absent. Column 39 gives the prior grant cell discarded before this row, or UINT64_MAX. Kind 3 is a kernel re-selection, with the crossing sample in column 2 and fresh decision in column 3. Its source frame is the rejected candidate, its source occupancy is zero, and no user exception or hardware IRQ occurred there. Unentered rows have zero return stamp; their debit/credit snapshot is a proposal until a delivery in the cell. A superseded proposal is followed by an explicit rollback if its cell expired. Ordinary earlier rows keep the original cause, register result and timestamps.

최초 산발 서버 일반 실행의 124번 기록은 그대로 보존한다. 수정판은 사용자 또는 유휴 복귀 직전에 실제 카운터를 다시 읽고 격자·기상 경계에 이르면 후보 프레임을 유지한 채 재선택한다. 미실행 후보에는 CPU 시간을 부과하지 않으며 칸이 지나면 해당 가차감과 보충 예약도 취소한다. v2 기록은 거부 시각, 실제 복귀·미실행·종료 구분 및 취소된 칸을 추가한다. 최종 표본에서 실제 ERET/유휴 분기까지의 명령 실행 시간은 남는다.
