# Periodic overrun acceptance design

CHG-030 / IMP-028 extends POL-004 and issues #28, #30 and #31. EVD-028 is planned. Apply REQ-002/005/023, DES-003/012 and VER-002/005/023. [한국어](m1-periodic-overrun.md)

Two 26-tick epochs share one physical counter origin O and quantum Q. Tick k is O+kQ. DM starts at k=26. Within each epoch A has period 10 and deadline 9, B period 12 and deadline 4. Each has nominal cost 1. The [host RTA](fp-response.en.md) recurrence with zero additional costs, blocking, jitter and IRQ demand gives RM bounds A=1, B=2 and DM bounds B=1, A=2. Both fit. The injected A generation 1 deliberately violates its cost. These conditional bounds do not measure QEMU service or board WCET.

Release three generations per task at their original periodic boundaries. At each IRQ first commit any previously submitted exact-boundary completion, then report each unfinished due job once, then release arrivals and select by RM or DM. A0 executes a finite loop and submits its result by SVC before tick 9. Its logical completion takes effect exactly at 9. Keep the physical SVC sample and the IRQ observation separately. A1 loops with IRQs enabled until tick 21, then completes by SVC strictly past its physical deadline at 19. At 20 A2 enters the backlog with its own release and deadline while A1 retains its stack and generation. Only the oldest unfinished generation of each task can execute. B1 preempts A1 at 12 under DM. Under RM B1 waits and misses at 16. After A1 finishes, complete all remaining jobs. End each epoch at 25 with six releases, six completions and RM two misses or DM one.

Independently decode the symbol periodic_overrun_witness. Compare releases, submissions, commits, misses and selections against the common calendar, sample physical counter values, verify IRQ and return PCs against ELF symbols and track progress across resumed frames. Count one completion and at most one miss per identity. QEMU virtual counter observations cover this finite single-CPU injection. Board interrupt latency, WCET and physical deadline equality require board evidence.

## Capture and journal layout

On Linux run `PERIODIC_OVERRUN_CAPTURE_DIR=/tmp/overrun-01 python3 tools/qemu_boot.py --profile periodic-overrun --kernel "$ELF" --qemu tools/periodic_overrun_capture.py` with a fresh capture directory and the target ELF. Decode audit.bin as little-endian u64 values. The four-word header contains O, Q, counter frequency in Hz and the number of committed rows. Each nine-word row contains global tick, event, slot, nominal release tick, absolute deadline tick, physical sample, PC, x20 progress and exception-frame address. Slot = 2 × generation + task (A=0, B=1), reused only between epochs. Idle slot 6 uses UINT64_MAX for release and deadline. A zero frame denotes a calendar-only event. Event codes are IRQ (1), release (2), miss (3), SVC submission (4), logical completion (5), and selected return (6). IRQ events and their calendar transitions share the counter sampled in the handler. Return records sample separately before assembly restores the selected frame. For A0 only, completion has the deadline tick and the IRQ sample. For other completions the tick labels the current interval and the physical SVC sample gives the completion observation. Expect 52 IRQs, 12 releases, 12 submissions, 12 completions, 3 misses and 63 selections. The terminal IRQ parks without a selection.

The launcher runs a local Python capture script with its current Python interpreter, including when the checkout uses Windows line endings.
